# Consciousness and existence — executive summary

**Version 1 · Released after independent review and G3 adjudication.** The scoped project cycle is complete. The findings remain provisional, and methodological acceptance does not establish a metaphysical theory as true.

A conscious foundation offers a substantive conditional way to connect subjective experience, concrete existence and enduring significance. It could gain explanatory unity if its grounding, individuation and continuity relations receive independent warrant. The reviewed corpus does not establish the complete package or identify a winner among the strongest versions of all alternatives.

| Component | Provisional assessment |
| --- | --- |
| Fundamentality | Serious philosophical motivations remain contested; an explanatory gap alone does not settle basic ontology. |
| Universal conscious ground | Conditional unification is possible; distinct subjects and constrained embodiment need further explanation. |
| Brain independence | The selected acquisition cases do not establish the required timing and exclusion. Local neural causal contributions have positive evidence within measured scope. |
| Personal survival | Same-person subjectivity beyond irreversible bodily death is not established by the assessed resuscitation reports. |
| Timelessness | A timeless ground with temporal persons is a conditional model; an actual non-successive bearer is unestablished. |
| Preservation | A record, lasting value and continuing subjectivity are different targets. Informational persistence alone does not establish survival. |
| Intention | Specified purposes can have conditional explanatory value; actual agency and comparative expectations need independent warrant. |
| Goodness | Richer perfect-governance and perfect-love proposals face substantive suffering and hiddenness challenges. Restoration does not alone justify permission of harm. |
| Moral authority | A normative bridge is required. The limited original-source comparison does not establish unique conscious grounding of obligation. |

The strongest positive case concerns a potentially shared explanation of several connected questions. The strongest countercase combines measured neural contributions, unresolved acquisition timing, live philosophical alternatives and the additional identity and normative burdens of the richer proposal. Neither case settles every question.

The executed sensitivity tests identify which reasons depend on particular cases, proxies and premises. Removing a case narrows its contribution; rejecting one bridge disables that route without automatically disproving the conclusion. Stable broad uncertainty labels are not evidence that the whole package is robust. No numerical posterior or source-count weighting was used.

Worthwhile continuation could enhance significance under stated value conditions. Its desirability does not establish that it occurs or that finite lives lack meaning. Likewise, a shared origin does not by itself establish intention, goodness or moral duties.

Independent R18 review accepted the scoped synthesis with one terminology correction, now applied in the release: **CF0 describes conscious life occurring without stipulated purpose; CF1 specifies intentional production.** No evidence or confidence judgment changed. All twelve limits on stronger conclusions remain binding.
