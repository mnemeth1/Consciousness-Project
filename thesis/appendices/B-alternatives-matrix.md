<!-- id: apB-p001 -->
This appendix sets the six families of positions side by side under the same six questions, and it does so under a rule that must be stated before any cell is read. Every comparison cell below is a project reconstruction. It was authored at the synthesis stage from listed accepted anchors, and only the exact statements of those anchors are reviewed author attributions. No cell is an author's own verdict on his or her position, no cell count is a score, and no row is ranked against another. The synthesis constraints require each cell to be marked as an inspected original, a secondary attribution, a project reconstruction or an uninspected variant, and they forbid any broad strongest-family ranking while the original-defender gaps in Appendix C remain open. The first withheld gate stands on exactly this appendix. The six questions are: coherence (C1); explanatory scope (C2); independent warrant and cost (C3); fit with the inspected evidence (C4); what could discriminate (C5); and remaining problems and the stopping point (C6). Entries under C5 are proposed test criteria, not demonstrated predictions.

<!-- id: apB-p002 -->
Three things should be understood about the cells before any of them is read. First, the C1 to C6 cells below are the synthesis-stage reconstructions, printed as frozen version 1 text. They were written before Phase 2 supplied inspected originals and actual replies for several families, so a cell may state a family in a weaker form than chapter 3's strongest inspected formulation. For that reason the cells may not be used to grade any family against chapter 3. Chapter 3 carries the comparison at that level. The coverage line of each row below records what chapter 3 records about which exchanges are inspected originals, which positions are inspected without full reply chains, which remain secondary attributions, and which exact versions remain uninspected. Better coverage narrows a reconstruction's distance from the authors; it does not convert a reconstruction into an authored conclusion. Second, the purpose labels in the creator row follow the accepted correction from the synthesis stage: CF0 is production, CF1 is intentional production, and development and preservation are the further specified ends. A different reading of CF0, as the descriptive occurrence of conscious life with no purpose prediction, appears in two places. The accepted purpose hypotheses of the meaning packet state it, and the submitted P2S01 analysis reuses it. Where this appendix leans on P2S01's use of that reading, it is labeled as a rebased input, closed at its rebased scope by the release adjudication and never an accepted finding. Third, one cell has changed against the synthesis-stage text, and only one: the creator row's C5 cell dropped production from its list of tested purpose variants, and it is restored below to the accepted wording. The families overlap. Created persons can have a physical, psychophysical or dualist ontology; mental grounding can be plural or universal; a creator need not be identical with created persons; and the moral theories cross-cut all six.

## Common rules and the Phase 2 update

<!-- id: apB-p010 -->
Four rules govern every cell. Every comparison cell is a project reconstruction, with original attribution claims separately addressable through their anchor records. The six ontological families are not the four moral families. No total ordering and no numeric posterior is performed, and equal open labels are not equal credibility. And a basic entity or law may be an explanatory stopping point in any family, so independent adjustable properties and rules are counted symmetrically. The submitted P2S01 analysis, used here as a labeled rebased input and not as an accepted finding, updated all thirty-six cells against the Phase 2 packets with explicit before-and-after records, applied the neural and controlled-acquisition constraints symmetrically, and selected no six-family winner; the submitted P2S02 execution, likewise labeled, retained the thirty-six cells under both original-restriction conditions as project comparisons. The cells printed above are not that update; they are the frozen version 1 text, with the single C5 correction stated above, and the P2S01 rewrite of them stands outside this appendix. The release adjudication closed that analysis at its rebased scope without promoting its artifacts to accepted findings (state/acceptance_P3G2.json), and the cells stay the frozen version 1 text under the rule stated at the head of this appendix, which rests on a recorded owner decision (D5, cited in the crosswalk entries for apB-p002 and for this paragraph). Nothing in either analysis, and nothing in this appendix, ranks the families.
