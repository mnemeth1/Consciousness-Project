<!-- id: apC-p001 -->
This register does one thing that the rebase amendment requires and that no chapter can do for itself. It gives every one of the thirty-five historical gap groups and every one of the twelve withheld stronger-conclusion gates exactly one explicit disposition. The amendment allows three kinds: resolved by cited reviewed evidence, addressed by a bounded Phase 3 gap packet with its outcome recorded, or a standing limitation stated in the relevant chapter. A generic caveat does not satisfy a row, and an administrative status never closes an evidence gap. Two facts about this register should be understood before reading it. First, no Phase 3 gap packet was dispatched, because no chapter attempted a conclusion that a packet would have been needed to license; so no row carries the second kind of disposition. Second, the dispositions below are the author's proposals to the coverage gate. Only the overseer records a disposition, and only the coverage gate can accept coverage; nothing in this appendix closes a gap or opens a gate. The coverage gate adopted these dispositions on 14 September 2026 after mechanical verification of every named paragraph (state/acceptance_P3G1.json; work/P3G1/disposition_register.json). A later semantic re-check of every row narrowed or re-pointed eight paragraph lists and added the missing statement to four chapter paragraphs, recorded as amendment P3G1-A1 of the same record; no disposition changed. The gate's record, not this appendix, is the disposition of record.

<!-- id: apC-p002 -->
Each gap row is built from three layers. The historical wording of the gap, its closure condition and its priority come from the canonical register fixed at the synthesis audit. The Phase 2 status comes from the accepted methods audit, which split most gaps into narrow subrequirements and recorded, for each, whether it was resolved, partly addressed, access-unresolved, deferred with the stronger claim withheld, already resolved, or a persistent audit loss; those audit labels describe requirement statuses, not evidential weights. The thesis disposition then names the category and the chapter paragraphs where the limitation is actually stated, so that the coverage gate can check the statement rather than take it on trust. Three rows are proposed as resolved by cited reviewed evidence at their inspected scope. Thirty-one are proposed as standing limitations stated in a chapter. One, the historical audit loss, is a standing limitation that no repair can ever close, and it is stated as such in the methodology chapter.
