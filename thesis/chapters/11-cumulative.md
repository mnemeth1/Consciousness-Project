<!-- id: c11-p001 -->
We have now walked through ten chapters, and it is time to ask the question the whole thesis exists to answer. When the pieces are put together, do they add up to more than they are worth separately? This chapter answers that question in the only way the project's method allows. It lays out the dependence map, so that the reader can see which conclusions rest on which observations, testimony and premises. It restates the eight sensitivity tests that were fixed in advance at the synthesis audit and executed at the accepted synthesis stage, re-reads each against chapters 3 through 10, and reports what each test does to each of the nine hypothesis components. It states the strongest cumulative case for a conscious foundation and the strongest case against it, each at full strength. And it closes with the limitations that bound everything said here. This is the core of the synthesis contract that Phase 2 left unfinished (P2S03), now discharged through the thesis under the recorded rebase. Two submitted analyses from that phase, P2S01 and P2S02, are used in what follows, and every use is labeled. They were submitted analyses. The release adjudication later closed them at their rebased scope without promoting their artifacts to accepted findings (state/acceptance_P3G2.json), so they enter here as labeled rebased inputs and never as findings.

<!-- id: c11-p002 -->
Let me say at the outset what a cumulative assessment cannot mean here. It cannot mean pooling. No posterior probability is computed anywhere in this chapter, because the denominators, the disjointness of samples and the likelihoods that such a number would require were never established. It cannot mean averaging, since the three judgments the method keeps apart (how reliable a record is, how strong an inference from it is, and what confidence a component warrants) are not numbers and are never combined. And it cannot mean counting. A reused premise counted once per chapter, or a retold case counted once per publication, would manufacture the appearance of convergence without its substance. The synthesis constraints forbid each of these moves by name, and this chapter is written under them. What remains is a qualitative, conditional comparison: what the selected evidence and arguments can and cannot establish, and why.

## Can the pieces add up?

<!-- id: c11-p003 -->
There is a serious objection to the whole restricted-unification approach, and it deserves to be stated before anything else. Scientific and philosophical theories often gain support as packages. We do not demand that every component of a theory be confirmed on its own before we allow evidence to confirm the conjunction. A conscious-ground theory could, in principle, explain experiential character, lawful neural mediation, distinct subjects and purposive order together, and its economy might compensate for uncertainty about any single mechanism. To insist on an independent demonstration of each connection would prejudge that legitimate strategy against the proposal. The article accepts this objection, and so does this thesis. The relevant standard is constraint, not evidential isolation.

<!-- id: c11-p004 -->
But notice what the concession requires. Shared evidence favors a joint model when the model's commitments constrain one another and expose it to failure. Convergence becomes evidential only when the connections among the commitments are specified well enough to distinguish the proposal from its rivals. That is the standard announced in the introduction, and it is the standard applied here. It has teeth for a simple reason. Two things imitate the profile of independent support without its content: a premise reused in several domains and credited each time, and an attribute added to a theory precisely so that it can absorb recalcitrant evidence. The dependence map exists to catch the first. The sensitivity tests exist to catch the second.

## The dependence map

<!-- id: c11-p005 -->
What is the map? It is a record of which conclusions depend on which observations, which testimony, which philosophical premises and which identity and value assumptions. It was built at the synthesis stage as thirty-one inference routes toward the nine components and four cross-cutting targets, and the full map lives in Appendix D. Two rules govern how it is read. Within a route, the required inputs are conjunctive: every one must hold for the route to deliver its conclusion. Across routes, separate supporting routes for the same component are alternatives, and nothing about them is assumed to be statistically independent or exhaustive. A third rule matters most. Several of the positive routes are condition maps rather than evidence. A route that says "one conscious ground with distinct dependent subjects" partly states the thing being investigated. A map of what must work is not evidence that it does work.

<!-- id: c11-p006 -->
Consider what the evidential base actually contains once the dependence accounting of the earlier chapters is taken seriously. The phenomenal chapter rests on four conversations: the Chalmers and Papineau exchange over the explanatory gap, the Frankish and Nida-Rümelin exchange over illusionism, the emergence exchange among O'Connor and Wong, Kim and Shoemaker, and the constitution exchange between Strawson and Goff. The existence chapter rests on one modern cosmological exchange, two historical texts read in modernized English, one order-and-intelligibility essay that shares its assumptions with the cosmological argument's attribute stage, and one fine-tuning literature recorded as a single shared dependency group. The empirical chapter's celebrated cases each reduce to one narrator-rooted reporting chain, and its prospective studies keep their study-level sample groups. The continuity, meaning, morality and goodness chapters are dominated by shared philosophical premises and per-author groups. The thesis cites well over a hundred sources. It does not thereby possess over a hundred confirmations of anything.

<!-- id: c11-p007 -->
The synthesis audit fixed fourteen dependence groups, and every one of them survives into this chapter. Neal's river and intensive-care episodes share one narrator. Alexander's illness and recovery share one patient and one narrative. AWARE I is one study; AWARE II's prospective arm and its selected community arm are one article with different ascertainment; an excerpt and a full text of the same paper are one publication. The Dutch follow-ups reuse one cohort, and the dentures anecdote's appearance in the cohort paper is a documentary link, not cohort membership. The three Bede narratives share one literary lineage. The India collection and the Japanese reanalysis are different sources whose prompted categories cannot be pooled. The IANDS and Liège collections may overlap in unknown ways, and the DMT study repeats thirteen volunteers under two conditions. The macaque trials, the shared anesthesia cohort and the single stimulated patient contain repeated observations. The Cogitate protocol, results, supplement, critique and correction concern one experiment. The dentures and Reynolds debates are advocate, critic and reply multiplicity around one event each. And three philosophical groups cut across the chapters: a shared Chalmers and Papineau lineage, shared explanatory premises (sufficient reason, explanatory completeness, necessary ground, primitive stopping points), and shared identity and value premises. A fourteenth group names the whole common-foundation proposal, whose unification may have explanatory value without multiplying independent evidence.

<!-- id: c11-p008 -->
The premises that the full package adds to the minimal thesis are thirteen, and they are worth naming because they are the currency of the whole comparison. Three concern the ground itself: a grounding relation from foundation to world, an individuation relation that yields distinct subjects, and a neural mapping that recovers the intervention evidence. Three concern persons: a death boundary and a same-person relation for survival, and a timelessness relation for the eternal variant. Two concern design: an agency relation and a warranted expectation. Two concern meaning: a value premise and an enhancement premise. Three concern the moral crown: a good-nature premise, an authority relation and a permission account. These are the added commitments of the strongest integrated route, and the map counts each of them once. Where the same premise recurs in two components, it is one premise with two uses. Where a premise supports both the proposal and the objection, as the value premises do in the morality and goodness chapters, neither side may count it twice.

<!-- id: c11-p009 -->
Reuse of a single evidential item across components is the other thing the map exposes. A life that motivates a survival hope, grounds a claim about meaning and figures in a design argument is one item of evidence, not three. Restoration assumed in order to defend goodness cannot then be counted as evidence for survival. A life-valuing preference assumed in order to interpret the evidence of life cannot then return as independent evidence of the designer's goodness. The desire for enduring significance is not evidence that endurance occurs. And the phenomenal-gap premise used for fundamentality, for unity and for purpose is one premise, not three observations. The synthesis stage wrote these four prohibitions into the map as circularity rules. This chapter treats them as binding.

<!-- id: c11-p010 -->
Now the point about condition maps can be made concrete. Of the thirty-one routes, eight partly restate their own targets: the universal-ground route, the survival route, the timeless-foundation route, the preservation-of-records route, the restoration route, the good-nature-and-authority route, the conscious-explanation-of-existence route and the full integrated route. Each of them says, in effect, what would have to be true for the component to hold. They are valuable. They tell us exactly where the burdens lie. But they cannot be credited as support, because a premise that specifies the conclusion earns no independent evidential increment by being stated. The remaining routes carry evidence or argument in the ordinary sense, and they do so on both sides. On the proposal's side stand the phenomenal-gap route, the four named-case and prospective routes and the order route; each carries recorded evidence inputs, and each is incomplete or conditional rather than empty. On the constraint side stand the neural-intervention route and the timing route, and beside them the identity counterproposal, the presence objection to timelessness, the evil and hiddenness challenges and the naturalist moral rival, which are philosophical challenges rather than observations. Where, then, are the discriminating observations, the ones in which a controlled contrast bears on how experience depends on the brain? In the neural-intervention records. The prospective studies supply measured flow counts and recalled reports whose timing and opportunity intersections are still missing, so their observations do not yet reach the component. That asymmetry is not an artifact of how the map was drawn. It is a fact about where the discriminating observations currently lie, and it leaves the positive routes standing as evidence with their recorded limits, not as nothing.

<!-- id: c11-p011 -->
Phase 2 changed the map's attribution, not its structure. This is the first place the submitted P2S01 analysis enters, and it enters as a labeled rebased input. That analysis updated all thirty-one routes and all thirty-six comparison cells against the nineteen accepted Phase 2 packets, recorded exact before-and-after states for each, and left the nine components, the four composites, the thirty-five gap dispositions and the seventy-nine narrow requirements intact. Its own summary of the principal change is worth repeating: better attribution to developed positions, serious criticisms and actual replies, together with more precise source-version, clinical, timing and sample limits. Better attribution is a real gain. It is not confirmation, and P2S01 does not claim it is. The chapters of this thesis were drafted from the accepted Phase 2 records directly, so the dependence accounting above stands on accepted material; the submitted analysis is cited here only for the observation that the map's shape held.

## Executed sensitivity tests

<!-- id: c11-p012 -->
Robustness is tested here by withdrawal, not by weighting. Each test removes a case, a class of cases, a premise or a bridge from a common baseline and asks which reasons remain for each of the nine components. The baseline is the one the accepted synthesis stage fixed: nine component assessments with their claim and argument anchors, three judgments kept separate, no numbers. The designs were prespecified at the synthesis audit; the accepted T17 execution ran them as seventeen scenarios on the version 1 evidence base. This chapter does not run a new execution, and it does not claim one. It restates the accepted T17 execution, re-reads each scenario against the fuller evidence of chapters 3 through 10, which incorporate the accepted Phase 2 packets, and notes where a Phase 2 addition enters a remove or retain list. The submitted P2S02 execution on the updated graph is cited beside it as a labeled rebased input. Because that analysis applies the same eight designs to the same Phase 2 material, its agreement with this chapter is agreement, not independent confirmation. One rule holds throughout. A withdrawal changes how a record may be used. It never changes the record's reliability, its reported values or its provenance.

<!-- id: c11-p013 -->
The first design removes the strongest disputed named near-death case. Because the strength ordering is ambiguous, the design requires a second candidate, and both were run. Reynolds was selected for unusually specific reported surgical detail and an inspected multi-party technical dispute over hearing. The dentures case was selected under a different criterion: a distinctive object-location report attributed through an identified nurse. The two were also removed together. Only two components move. Removing Reynolds withdraws that case's specific perception claims and its hearing dispute from both positive and negative use, and leaves the dentures, Alexander and prospective material with their own unresolved timing and exclusion chains. Removing the dentures case withdraws the object-location warrant and its sensory counteraccount, without touching the Dutch cohort's counts. Removing both leaves the additional-case convergence unavailable while the neural evidence survives. For survival, each removal takes away a resuscitation illustration that never crossed the irreversible-death boundary, so the requirement stands exactly as before. The Phase 2 reinspections of both cases added replies, corrections and a self-performed sound demonstration. They added no observation, so the updated result is the same: the named-case positive case becomes narrower, no baseline label changes, and a removed case's ordinary-mechanism dispute leaves with it. A possible mechanism is never kept as the explanation of a case that is no longer in the assessment.

<!-- id: c11-p014 -->
The second design removes every retrospective case and narrative, including the selected retrospective community arm, while retaining the truly prospective observations at their real denominators. Out go Neal, Alexander, Reynolds, the dentures case, the Bede narratives, Heim's 1892 collection of fall survivors, which chapter 6 uses as its second historical anchor, the India and Japan collections, the distressing-experience correspondence, the two IANDS comparison collections and AWARE II's community narratives. What stays is AWARE I, the AWARE II prospective arm, the Dutch cohort, the early Southampton study added in Phase 2 at its prospective denominator only, and the DMT study's within-person drug-and-placebo contrast without its retrospective comparator. Six components register a change. Universal subjecthood loses every motif-universality appeal and every contrary cultural description, since the same collections supplied both; the original grounding arguments remain, and no empirical universality was established at baseline. Brain independence loses the anomalous-detail material and the selection and cultural counterevidence together; what remains is prospective recruitment with missing opportunity and timing intersections, and prospective recruitment still yields recalled reports rather than time-stamped experience. Survival gains nothing, because the remaining resuscitated reports still do not cross the boundary. Timelessness loses its historical and selected duration reports. Intention loses retrospective testimony to cosmic purpose, symmetrically with its critical interpretation. Goodness loses both the pleasant and the distressing otherworld descriptions, and removing distressing reports verifies nothing about benevolent governance. The lesson of this test is the one chapter 6 anticipated: the favorable recurrence arguments and the cultural countercomparisons shrink together.

<!-- id: c11-p015 -->
The third design withdraws contributions whose sample or event dependence is uncertain. The IANDS and Liège collections, the retrospective comparator in the DMT study and the shared historical comparisons may overlap in participants; the map does not know. So their comparative convergence use is withdrawn, without declaring anyone a duplicate and without discarding any within-sample description. Three components change. No independent motif-convergence argument survives for universal subjecthood. For brain independence, the cross-context recurrence appeal and any pooled comparison-based countercase are withdrawn together, and the remaining individual reports and prospective constraints do not establish extracerebral acquisition. For timelessness, cross-sample recurrence of time-item endorsements is no longer usable, while the item's ambiguity and the within-person drug result still challenge any inference from phenomenology to ontology. Phase 2 sharpened this test rather than changing it: the Charland-Verville historical table is now recorded as documented analytic reuse, and the Bede narratives remain non-additive without being called one event.

<!-- id: c11-p016 -->
The fourth design varies the identity and preservation criteria, and it is the test that most clearly shows what a conceptual choice can and cannot do. Five identity targets were crossed with four of the recorded time-and-identity models, the third of them in a finite and an enlarged form. Psychological continuity with a branching rule, organism persistence with a cessation boundary, a simple subject with an independently warranted bearer, strict numerical identity as the unchanged target, and prudential successor concern were each run through temporal continuation, a timeless foundation with temporal persons, whole-history inclusion, and records-without-subject. Six components change, and they do not all move in one direction. Changing the criterion changes what would count as survival; no variant supplies actual beyond-death subjectivity. Adopting a simple-subject criterion is not itself evidence that a brain-independent realization obtains. And choosing an identity theory neither establishes nor disproves a non-successive foundation, since the timeless option can still place temporal persons under one. Successor concern changes the target and cannot be relabeled survival. Records-without-subject permits preservation of information and value with no survival at all. A preserving or developmental purpose changes its adequacy conditions with the target, since preserving records, successors or the same persons are different ends. And victim-specific restoration requires the victim, not a resembling successor. Phase 2 added the tenseless-persons exchange and the revised eternity relation; both refine the options and neither becomes a postmortem observation.

<!-- id: c11-p017 -->
The fifth design removes the central bridges of the meaning and morality chapters one at a time. Reject the premise that endurance is required for every kind of significance, and nothing happens to any of the nine components, because no supporting route in the map ever used it; enhancement and finite value remain separate live claims, and a preference for enduring significance remains no evidence of its actuality. Reject the bridge from unity to obligation, and the moral-authority component loses one proposed extension while descriptive universal subjecthood is untouched and no nihilism follows. Reject the bridge from consciousness or creation to legitimate authority, and the good-nature route can no longer ground binding authority, goodness can no longer be inferred from commands, and yet the value premises, the suffering and hiddenness arguments and the naturalist alternative all remain in place. The Phase 2 materials on finite meaning and on moral grounding clarify these distinctions; they do not reverse any of them.

<!-- id: c11-p018 -->
The sixth design removes the global explanatory, modal and designer-preference premises separately. Without a global principle of sufficient reason, the route from a necessary ground to an experiential ground is disabled, one upstream motivation for a mental foundation is lost, and the explanation-of-existence target becomes a conditional proposal with no demand behind it; the phenomenal-gap route is untouched, and no physical identity follows. Without the modal bridge from conceivability to possibility, the gap route is disabled and ideal conceivability may no longer be used to infer non-grounding; the phenomenal target remains, the ground route remains conditional on its own premises, and identity still needs positive warrant. Without independently warranted life-valuing preferences, the order route loses its differential expectation. Observed order and life then cannot support that particular designer inference, and no life-valuing preference is available for a benevolent-designer story. Order and life survive as things to be explained, and the failure of one abductive bridge proves neither the absence of purpose nor the absence of an agent. In every case, rejecting a bridge disables a route. It does not establish the component's negation.

<!-- id: c11-p019 -->
The seventh design tests the countercase with the same severity, by removing the strongest selected neural intervention and weakening the report-to-experience proxies. Remove the human fusiform stimulation case and the content-specific causal warrant becomes thinner, while the macaque arousal result, the anesthesia associations and the visual-encoding constraints remain. Remove the macaque case instead and the direct animal state-intervention warrant is lost, while the human results remain. Weaken the bridge from human report to phenomenal experience, and every human report loses inferential force at once: the neural correlations become less direct evidence of phenomenal realization, and the crisis reports, prospective and named, must receive exactly the same reduced credit rather than an asymmetric upgrade. Weaken the bridge from macaque arousal to awareness, and the animals' behavioral and neural changes remain documented while claims to know their phenomenology recede. Here is the symmetry the design was built to enforce. A weaker neural countercase is not evidence for fundamentality; weaker measurement is not evidence of extracerebral awareness; proxy skepticism is not proof that the animals lacked experience. Nothing in the seventh test moves any component toward the proposal.

<!-- id: c11-p020 -->
The eighth design restricts every claim about authors to directly inspected positive original defenders and their direct replies, and it is the only design that touches all nine components. Under it, only the exact inspected texts support their authors' positions; project reconstructions of missing rivals remain labeled reasoning, never defeated opponents; and no family ranking is calculated, because a selection of originals shaped by access is not a representative sample. Phase 2 changed this test more than any other, and honestly the change is a narrowing of exclusions rather than a lifting of them. The illusionism, causal-emergence, constitution, substance-dualist, plural-idealist, cosmological, fine-tuning, eternity, human-identity, finite-meaning, constructivist, robust-realist, horrendous-evils and hiddenness exchanges are now inspected originals with actual replies where the record says so. What remains outside the inspected set is recorded just as plainly: the exact 2006 Strawson text, any Kastrup reply to the selected Chalmers objection, the developed indispensability argument at Enoch's missing pages, the 2013 Metz chapter, the 1990 Adams print, and the wider defender and reply chains behind several families. The submitted P2S02 execution, cited here as a labeled input, ran this design twice on the updated graph, once as inherited and once with an added restriction that withdraws uses depending on missing editions, unauthenticated audio or unobserved clinical intersections; its reported result is narrower permitted use, not the deletion of any established event or component.

<!-- id: c11-p021 -->
What do the seventeen scenarios show when taken together? In none of them does a baseline confidence label cross a threshold. It would be a mistake to read that as robustness. The labels are stable because the stronger actuality thresholds were not met at baseline: a component that was never established cannot be dis-established by removing a case. What the tests actually change is which conditional reasons survive, and that is the useful result. The named-case tests narrow the anomalous-detail case and remove its disputes with it. The retrospective and dependence tests remove the favorable recurrence arguments and the cultural countercomparisons together. The identity test shows that no criterion manufactures a postmortem subject. The bridge tests show which routes each disputed premise carries and which it does not. The neural tests show that the countercase, too, can be weakened without the proposal gaining anything. And the originals test shows that every remaining ranking depends on rivals whose strongest formulations were never inspected. The submitted P2S02 analysis reached the same reading on the updated graph, reporting a change in the scope and force of particular arguments with no identified confidence threshold crossed. This chapter's re-reading of the accepted scenarios against chapters 3 through 10 agrees with it. Since both apply the same designs to the same material, that is one reading reached twice, not two confirmations.

## Holistic support and the constraint standard

<!-- id: c11-p022 -->
Why, then, does the package not yet earn holistic support? The article's diagnosis is precise, and the ten chapters have confirmed it item by item. Basic experience does not yet specify how subjects combine or differentiate; the constitution exchange ended with the positive burden undischarged, and the universal reversal owes the same individuation account. A necessary explanation does not settle the kind of explaining entity; the cosmological exchange delivers, at its conditional maximum, a necessary concrete ground, with mind on the far side of a bridge it does not build. Neural mediation is often asserted without a content-specific account; the transmission reply to the stimulation evidence survives logically and explains nothing in particular. Actual restoration is used to answer harms before there is evidence of its occurrence; the restoration defense presupposes the same-person continuation that the survival record does not supply. Each of these links can be coherently proposed. Their present flexibility is exactly what weakens the claimed gain in discrimination.

<!-- id: c11-p023 -->
What would turn the conjunction into a joint theory? The methodology chapter gave the general answer: commitments that constrain one another and could embarrass the theory, with predicted fit tracked separately from accommodated fit. The meaning chapter gave the template. A specified purpose must declare its population, means, constraints and tradeoffs in advance, because an outcome weakens a model only when the model's fixed commitments excluded it. The goodness chapter applied the same discipline to benevolence: a benevolent unification must let its claims about goodness restrict what counts as an expected world. The same demand applies to the whole. A conscious-ground theory that fixed its individuation rule, its neural mapping, its identity relation and its declared purposes before the evidence came in could gain support from that evidence as a package. The proposal in the record has not yet done so, and this thesis does not pretend that it has.

<!-- id: c11-p024 -->
The sensitivity results explain the qualitative shape of the case, and the shape is the article's. Rejecting the modal or sufficient-reason premise weakens the corresponding grounding argument without resolving the experiential target. Removing the designer-preference assumption weakens a life-order inference without making life evidentially irrelevant under every possible model. Removing a retrospective crisis case reduces one empirical contribution without erasing the controlled stimulation evidence or the prospective observations. Rejecting immortality as necessary for all meaning leaves possible worthwhile continuation intact. The positive and negative reasons are partly connected and partly separable. Neither wholesale confirmation nor wholesale dismissal respects that structure, and a reader who wants one or the other will find that the map refuses to supply it.

<!-- id: c11-p025 -->
One cross-cutting question should not be lost in the component-by-component accounting, because the proposal's advocates rightly press it: why is there anything at all, and why these laws? A fundamental-experience thesis does not by itself answer it. Every foundation, physical, psychophysical, experiential or conscious, needs an explicit stopping point, and the comparison is fair only if the stopping points are priced symmetrically. Counting a conscious foundation as one entity while ignoring its separately adjustable laws, intentions and individuation relations would overstate its simplicity. Demanding an explanation of every conscious primitive while exempting physical primitives would be the same error in reverse. The record leaves the explanation of existence as a conditional conscious-ground proposal, with no independent demand that the totality must have such an explanation once the global sufficient-reason premise is set aside. It is a live question. It is not a settled point in anyone's favor.

## The strongest cumulative case

<!-- id: c11-p026 -->
Fairness requires that the strongest case be stated at full strength, and the synthesis constraints require that its possible unification gains be preserved rather than dismissed because likelihoods are uncalibrated. Here it is. Subjective experience presents an explanatory target that listing discriminations, reports and functions does not discharge. A theory that places experience in the basis of reality meets that target directly, and it does so from a positive philosophical motivation rather than from a reaction to unusual experiences. A common conscious basis could then explain why private experience and shared physical regularity coexist, provided it states how distinct subjects depend on the whole and how their embodied perspectives track physical interventions. A defended principle of sufficient reason could add a second question, why any concrete order exists, and answer it with a necessary foundation. Specified life-valuing intentions could give order and conscious life conditional qualitative support for a creator relative to a specified rival. Actual same-person continuation in worthwhile relationships could preserve goods that extinction would end, a purposive and good foundation could connect those goods with a larger reason for embodied life, and a defensible normative relation could connect that foundation to obligations. If these conditions acquired independent warrant, one set of grounding relations would be doing explanatory work across questions that are otherwise answered separately. That is a real prize.

<!-- id: c11-p027 -->
What the case gains, if it succeeds, is shared explanatory structure. What it does not gain is nine independent confirmations. The near-death details supply questions about acquisition; they do not presently establish the exclusion and timing steps that would carry the package. The design comparison supplies conditional qualitative support; it does not supply a measure, a designer preference established apart from the datum, or a posterior. The meaning material supplies enhancement under stated premises; it does not supply necessity. The restoration defense supplies a coherent account of what could become of victims; it does not supply an observed restoration or a justification of permission. Each contribution is genuine within its scope, and each is conditional. A conjunction of conditionals is a research programme. It is not yet a result.

<!-- id: c11-p028 -->
The four circularity prohibitions are what keep the strongest case honest, and they should be restated as the case's own discipline rather than as an opponent's objection. The positive case may not assume restoration to defend goodness and then cite the defended goodness as evidence of survival. It may not assume life-valuing preferences to interpret the evidence of life and then count the inferred designer as proof of those preferences. It may not treat the desire for enduring significance as evidence that endurance occurs. And it may not spend the phenomenal-gap premise three times, once each for fundamentality, unity and purpose. A version of the case that respects these rules is weaker than the versions one meets in popular presentation. It is also the only version that could ever earn holistic support.

## The strongest countercase

<!-- id: c11-p029 -->
The countercase deserves the same treatment, and the synthesis constraints are specific about what it must combine. It begins with positive evidence, not with a list of objections. Stimulating a small region of one patient's cortex changed what a face looked like under sham and control contrasts; stimulating the central thalamus of two anesthetized macaques restored behavioral arousal and its markers. These are local causal contributions, and any surviving model must recover them. The strongest physical-identity response meets the explanatory gap directly rather than ignoring it: distinct phenomenal and physical concepts may pick out one property, and an identity once warranted needs no further explanation. Basic psychophysical laws offer a second live possibility, irreducible experience lawfully bound to embodied organization with no detachable person. Plural and impersonal grounds remain competitors for the universal one. The crisis evidence, for its part, never establishes the conjunction: in no inspected case are accurate content, an independently fixed interval and adequate process information established together, and the celebrated cases collapse under audit to single reporting chains whose decisive measurements were never made or never kept. Selection, long delays, unknown overlap and cultural variation weaken any inference from testimony to an afterlife. The identity, timelessness, intention and normative bridges all remain open. Suffering and nonresistant nonbelief challenge every specified perfect-governance package. Finite fitting engagement challenges the claim that significance requires endurance. And a developed naturalist route to moral realism stands alongside the divine-nature account, with the constructivist and robust-realist originals now inspected as well.

<!-- id: c11-p030 -->
Let me be equally clear about what the countercase does not establish, because the synthesis constraints forbid three inflations of it. It does not show that every crisis report has an ordinary cause. Naming a possible mechanism is not diagnosing a case, and in no dossier has a critic's mechanism been demonstrated as the actual cause of any particular report. It does not show that all hypotheses are equally credible. Unknown likelihoods are not equal likelihoods, and unresolved actuality is not equal probability. And it does not show that finite meaning exhausts meaning, that physicalism entails nihilism, that a complete neural explanation exists, or that any one family wins. That last restraint has a specific source: the countercase's strongest rivals are represented unevenly, and no broad winner may be declared by defeating a reconstruction of a rival whose strongest original was never read.

<!-- id: c11-p031 -->
So the comparison ends where the article said it would, in an asymmetry, and it is the chapter records, not the sensitivity re-reading, that earn each half of it. It supports embodied neural dependence more directly than metaphysical identity: the interventions are discriminating observations, while identity is a developed counterproposal that needs its own positive warrant. It supports finite meaningful engagement more directly than an all-encompassing preserving purpose: Wolf's account and the finite-value argument ground a kind of significance for which duration is not part of the explanation, while every preserving purpose in the record remains an unmeasured specification. It leaves phenomenal explanation contested, because the realist target and its representational reinterpretation are both in dispute and neither side possesses a completed account. And it leaves survival unresolved, because the empirical conjunction and the identity relation have not been established together, and a coherent identity criterion cannot substitute for either. These are conclusions about the selected evidence and arguments. They are not conclusions about the impossibility of a more discriminating model.

<!-- id: c11-p032 -->
The verdict of this chapter, then, is the restricted-unification thesis stated in the introduction, and it is worth saying exactly what now stands behind each half of it. The first half says that a conscious foundation is a serious candidate for improving the explanation of experience. What earns it is chapter 4: the record supplies a phenomenal explanatory demand, and a theory that puts experience among its primitives meets that demand by definition. But the same chapter fixes the condition. The relief counts as an advantage only if the demand has been correctly identified, and that is exactly what the identity and illusionist records contest. So the first half stands as a conditional claim whose condition is contested, and it is stated here in no stronger form than that. The second half says that the proposal's present motivation supports considerably less than a providential account of enduring personal significance. That half is earned outright, and it is earned in pieces. Chapter 4 leaves individuation undischarged. Chapter 5 delivers, at its conditional maximum, a necessary ground that does not yet yield mind. Chapter 6 finds that the neural interventions constrain every surviving model while the crisis conjunction is never established. ## Limitations

<!-- id: c11-p033 -->
The cumulative conclusions above concern the evidence and arguments the project selected, inspected and reviewed under bounded budgets and a frozen cutoff. They are not conclusions about the world beyond that record. Where the record is silent, this chapter has said that it is silent; where the record contains only a report, it has said so; where an argument turns on a premise, it has named the premise. A reader who rejects a conclusion here can locate, through the crosswalk, exactly which record or premise they are rejecting. That traceability is the method's promise, and it is also the method's ceiling.

<!-- id: c11-p034 -->
Access gaps bound the empirical half of the assessment, and several of them are decision-relevant. No raw chart, tracing, operative record or original interview recording was inspected for any case dossier. Several originals remain uninspected: the Sabom chapter at the root of the Reynolds chain, the 1991 article and the full 1994 transcript behind the dentures chain, the remaining pages of the 2010 Dutch response, the published Noyes and Kletti translation, the original 2011 Neal edition, the 2012 Alexander text and the AWARE II supplement. Four source requests remain pending in the frozen queue. Two purported transcripts remain audio-unverified. Some material was screened out or never selected rather than lost: the Moorjani account was screened and deferred and is never treated as a case in this thesis; the Sacks clinical comparator papers, the Leftow, Kvanvig and Wierenga defenses of timeless eternity, the direct critique of the Indian tunnel finding and its reply, and the Japanese interviews underlying the reanalysis were not inspected, and nothing here rests on them. The same is true of the wider cultural strata the register names, Asian historical, African, Melanesian, Indigenous and Muslim originals: they were never selected, so no cultural generalization in this thesis reaches them. The AWARE II author replies, one of which an earlier synthesis stage listed as its standing request, have since been inspected under Phase 2, and they supplied argument, not the missing participant, timing or exposure data. None of these gaps is evidence for the opposite ontology. Each of them is a reason to withhold a stronger inference.

Four further limitations travel with every conclusion. Source-version identity is unresolved for several load-bearing texts, and an attribution is only as exact as the version behind it. Case timing is retrospective throughout; no reported experience in the record is time-locked to a measured absence of processing. Sample and event dependence is pervasive, and the map treats unknown overlap as unknown rather than as either duplication or independence. And the philosophical bridge premises remain premises; accurate attribution of any of them establishes nothing about its truth. The procedural limits are just as real. The coverage gate, the whole-thesis adversarial review and the release adjudication have been run and recorded (state/acceptance_P3G1.json, state/acceptance_P3R20.json, state/acceptance_P3G2.json), and the final independent review that the article never received was discharged, without weakening, through those layers. But what they certify is method, review and stated limits, never the truth of a conclusion. The two submitted Phase 2 analyses used in this chapter were closed at their rebased scope by that adjudication, not accepted as findings, and they remain labeled inputs here. And the literature base is frozen at 11 September 2026, so later publications are logged as leads rather than evidence.

Let me be clear, finally, about what this chapter has not shown. It has not shown that consciousness is fundamental, or that it is not.
