## Abstract

<!-- id: c00-p001 -->
Does consciousness belong to the foundation of reality, and what would follow for existence, survival, meaning and morality if it did? A conscious foundation promises to connect the explanation of experience with the existence of an ordered world, the persistence of persons and the authority of value. This thesis tests that promise by critical comparative synthesis. Six families of positions are set against the same criteria, nine hypothesis components are assessed separately, and every substantive claim is traced through a paragraph-level crosswalk to a reviewed record or a labeled premise. Eight prespecified sensitivity tests, executed by withdrawal at the accepted synthesis stage, are re-read against the chapters. The result is a restricted unification. Experiential primitiveness answers, by definition, the demand that experience enter the fundamental explanatory vocabulary; whether that demand has been correctly identified remains contested, and the move transfers, rather than discharges, the problems of structure and subject individuation. Controlled neural intervention imposes substantive causal constraints on every surviving model, while the selected crisis evidence in no inspected case establishes together the accurate content, the fixed interval and the process information that discriminating extracerebral acquisition would require. Neither an eternal representation of a life nor the desirability of its continuation supplies personal survival. Finite meaningful engagement and practical normativity have resources that do not wait on a settled cosmic ontology, and a benevolent specification must let its goodness restrict what counts as an expected world. The verdict is asymmetric: positive evidence of embodied neural contribution in the tested intervention contexts, and finite significance supported under an explicit value premise, coexist with an unresolved phenomenal explanation and a viable, conditional conscious-foundation programme. Twelve stronger conclusions remain withheld behind recorded gates, and thirty-five evidence gaps receive explicit dispositions. Holistic support remains possible, but only for a constrained joint account whose connections do explanatory work rather than restate the desired conclusions.

## Declarations

<!-- id: c00-p002 -->
This manuscript is AI-assisted project research, prepared with AI assistance in research organization, argument analysis and drafting under the direction of the project owner. Chapter drafting, independent review and acceptance follow the project's recorded workflow, in which no author approves their own output and a distinct reviewer reopens decisive sources. Until those are recorded otherwise, this is a working draft.

The evidence base is bounded and frozen: publications through 11 September 2026, in English or accessible English translation, gathered under prespecified search and inspection budgets and inspected at identified versions with locators. No payment for access, no contact with witnesses or authors and no request for private records was made.
