<!-- id: c12-p001 -->
A conclusion should say plainly what has been shown, what has not, and what it would take to change the verdict. That is all this chapter does. It restates the restricted-unification thesis with the evidence of ten chapters behind it. It then assesses each of the nine hypothesis components on its own, keeping the three judgments the method requires apart: how reliable the records are, how strong the inferences from them are, and what confidence the component itself warrants. For each component it names the boundary the synthesis audit fixed and the observation or argument that would materially change the assessment. It adds no evidence. The nine assessments are those the accepted synthesis stage fixed as its baseline, checked here against the fuller chapters that now stand behind them; the submitted P2S02 reassessment on the updated evidence is cited, where it is cited, as a labeled rebased input and nothing more. There is no worldview score at the end of this chapter, because the evidence does not license one, and where the evidence does not discriminate this chapter says so rather than choosing.

## The restricted-unification thesis restated

<!-- id: c12-p002 -->
Here is the thesis in one breath. A conscious foundation earns serious consideration by confronting the experiential explanandum directly. That virtue is real in one definite sense: a theory with experience among its primitives no longer has to derive the experiential from a vocabulary stipulated to contain nothing experiential. Chapter 4 also fixed its price. The relief is by definition, and it counts as an advantage only if the explanatory demand has been correctly identified, which the identity and illusionist records contest. So the virtue is conditional, and the condition is open. The second half of the thesis is not conditional in the same way. The moment the proposal undertakes to explain an ordered physical world, private subjects, continued persons and binding reasons, its primitives must be joined by substantive accounts of structure, identity, agency and value. None of those accounts comes free with the word "consciousness." Chapters 4 through 10 examined each of those accounts against the record and found each still owed. The thesis is therefore neither a dismissal nor a down payment. It says that the proposal deserves the comparison it has received, and that the comparison leaves its most ambitious consequences with far less support than its starting point.

<!-- id: c12-p003 -->
The selected evidence gives guidance, and the guidance is asymmetric in a way that should now be familiar. The controlled stimulation findings constrain every account of human consciousness that survives; a theory must recover them, whatever its ontology. The crisis reports have not yet discriminated extracerebral acquisition, because in no inspected case are accurate content, a fixed interval and adequate process information established together. Finite engagement can matter without an established cosmic purpose, because the necessity thesis lacks a defended bridge while the enhancement thesis survives. And goodness makes providential claims answerable to suffering and hiddenness, because a specified benevolence generates expectations and expectations can be tested. Each of these four is a conclusion about the record. Each was earned in a chapter of its own, and each is bounded by what that chapter said it could not conclude.

<!-- id: c12-p004 -->
Two rules govern how the nine assessments below should be read. First, the labels are reasoned qualitative judgments, not points on a scale. "Unresolved" attached to two different components does not mean the two are equally likely; it means that in each case the stronger actuality threshold was not met, for reasons that differ and are stated. Second, separate assessment is not logical independence. If the numerically same person had subjectivity beyond irreversible death, that would entail the narrow preservation of that subjectivity; if survival occurred with no replacement substrate, that would entail an instance of brain independence. These consequences are recorded when the relevant version is selected, and none of them transfers confidence from one component to the package. A component that is not established does not become established because another is.

## Nine components, nine assessments

### H-F: fundamentality

<!-- id: c12-p005 -->
What do the records reliably support? That Chalmers distinguishes functional from phenomenal explanation and argues, conditionally on modal and epistemic bridges, against physicalism; that Papineau defends the phenomenal-concept strategy and challenges what an identity owes us; that the two disagree about inference rather than about whether there is a debate. These are exact attributions to inspected texts. What is the inference? Two conditional routes to basic experience, one from the phenomenal gap through a modal bridge and a basic-ontology premise, the other from a necessary concrete ground through a mentality premise, with a developed identity counterproposal standing against both. Neither route is an observation, and the two are not independent, since they share the fundamentality premise.

<!-- id: c12-p006 -->
The assessment is that fundamentality has contested positive philosophical motivation and unresolved actuality. The phenomenal target is a serious explanatory demand. The bridge from an epistemic gap to an ontological one, and the further choice of basic rather than strongly emergent experience, remain disputed at every joint. A required ultimate explanation, even if granted, does not by itself identify an experiential ground. The boundary fixed for this component is that irreducibility or a necessary ground does not establish one universal subject, and that the primitive ontology and the actual dependence relation each need argument. What would change the assessment? A defended modal bridge, or a constrained account of how basic experience generates the causal organization the interventions reveal, would strengthen it; a completed identity or misrepresentation account that preserved the explanandum would weaken it. Neither exists in the record.

### H-U: universal subjecthood

<!-- id: c12-p007 -->
The records reliably support Kastrup's dissociation-based proposal and Chalmers's subject-constitution objection as attributions, together with the plural Leibnizian alternative; the clinical illustrations behind the proposal were never inspected, and the rival original chains are incomplete. The inference is a conditional explanatory proposal: one conscious ground could unify private experience and public lawfulness if it stated how distinct subjects depend on the whole and how their embodied perspectives track interventions. But the universal-ground route partly specifies its own conclusion, and coherent unification is a potential explanatory gain, not confirmation of a universal subject. The assessment is a conditional explanatory proposal with no discriminating warrant for actuality. Fundamentality does not identify one subject or conscious whole; individuation and a constrained neural mapping are additional burdens; and the boundary is that shared motifs or a single designer do not establish numerical experiential unity. What would change it is a manifestation and individuation rule that could fail under neural or perceptual contrasts and did not. An appearance vocabulary that accommodates every outcome cannot earn that credit.

### H-B: brain independence

<!-- id: c12-p008 -->
This is the component with the most records behind it, and the records must be sorted by what they are. Study counts, reported technical disputes and prospective flow diagrams are supported records of what each paper says. Even at that level one conflict stays open: AWARE I prints 140 stage-one interviews beside 52 plus 90 and a table denominator of 142, and the AWARE II introduction cites 143; chapter 6 leaves the discrepancy unresolved, and so does this chapter. The truth of any reported event, and the patient-specific absence of neural processing during any reported experience, are not established to the same standard, and the AWARE II target-opportunity intersection remains unknown. On the positive side of the ledger stand the interventions: site-specific stimulation changed reported perceptual content in one patient under sham and control contrasts, and state-targeted stimulation restored arousal markers in two anesthetized macaques. On the other side stand four case routes, each requiring accurate report, time-lock, process exclusion and a specified dependence model together, and none delivering the full chain.

<!-- id: c12-p009 -->
The assessment is unresolved extracerebral acquisition alongside positive evidence for embodied neural contribution. Specific reports merit investigation. Independently fixed acquisition time and relevant process and information exclusion are missing in every inspected case, and prospective recruitment improves ascertainment without turning recollection into a real-time measurement. The boundary fixed for this component runs in both directions: brain influence is not the negation of every independence proposal, and absent measurement is not absent processing; an independence proposal needs a specified bearer and process and discriminating observations. What would change the assessment is stated exactly in the empirical chapter: an independently recorded report connected to a prespecified target exposure, individual physiology and a narrowly defined acquisition interval, with ordinary information access assessed. Neither a chart that cannot be obtained nor an interval that was never measured can be filled by inference, and a completed literature repair would clarify a debate without manufacturing a measurement.

### H-S: personal survival

<!-- id: c12-p010 -->
The identity authors are inspected attributions, and the case reports retain their narrow reliability; neither is a verified observation of anyone beyond death. The inference requires a death boundary and a same-person relation together, and those two conditions specify most of the target, so their conjunction explains what must be shown rather than supplying evidence that it has been. The assessment is that survival is not established by this corpus, with additional endpoint and identity gaps. Even a verified unusual acquisition in a resuscitated person would not establish same-person subjectivity beyond irreversible death, since resuscitation does not cross the endpoint. And each identity criterion demands different evidence. Psychological continuity must not presuppose the identity it proves. An organism that has irreversibly ceased needs a defended account of its return. A simple bearer must be independently warranted rather than stipulated. Successor concern is a different target. The boundary is that the endpoint and the identity standard may not be weakened. What would change the assessment is actual further same-person subjectivity together with a defensible identity criterion; a coherent model, an eternal representation of a life, or a valued successor would not.

### H-T: timelessness

<!-- id: c12-p011 -->
The time-model and philosophical attributions are reliable at their limited domains, and the duration reports are reports of phenomenology. The inference builds a model rather than discovering its actuality: a non-successive foundation grounding ordered temporal persons is conditionally intelligible, and the presence objection, that a cognitively perfect ground of a tensed world would need cognitive succession, is a conditional challenge that depends on objective presentness rather than a contradiction in every timeless model. The assessment is contested model coherence with actuality unestablished. Relativity, relational observables and reported timelessness identify no non-successive bearer; altered duration is not absence of intrinsic succession. The boundary is that neither the absence of an external time parameter nor a report of altered duration establishes a bearer without intrinsic succession. What would change the assessment is an account of a non-successive bearer and its relation to temporal contents that answered the presence objection on its own terms, or a decisive temporal test. A duration report cannot do that work.

### H-P: preservation

<!-- id: c12-p012 -->
The preservation distinctions are accepted conceptual analysis, not observations of a universal store. Records, value, relationships and continued subjectivity are different targets, and a record can be preserved while its owner ceases. The inference is that same-person survival, if it obtained, would entail the narrow preservation of that subjectivity, and that the entailment cannot be run backwards. The assessment is that the distinctions are supported while cosmic preservation remains unresolved in actuality. Finite records do not establish cosmic or permanent preservation, and a conscious foundation's awareness of a life is not automatically that life's subjectivity. The boundary is that the proposal must specify what persists, and that fidelity of a record is not survival. What would change the assessment is evidence for an actual bearer of preserved value or relation beyond the finite records the world already contains; what would not is the desirability of such a bearer.

### H-I: intention

<!-- id: c12-p013 -->
The order, selection and purpose arguments are accepted attributions and analyses; no measured likelihood of a designer choosing life exists in the corpus. The inference is a conditional abductive comparison: a specified candidate with independently constrained preferences could make order or conscious life more expected than under a specified rival. It is not a deduction to agency, and its preference and selection assumptions do material work. The assessment is conditional explanatory value with uncalibrated differential support. Order and conscious life alone do not identify intention; a universe that produces consciousness and a universe that exists in order to produce it are distinct hypotheses. The boundary is that production is not intended production, that prior ends, the level of the agent and the comparative expectations must be specified, and that a local designer does not imply a purpose for the containing cosmos. What would change the assessment is a declared purpose with population, means, constraints and tradeoffs fixed in advance and then borne out; preferences read off the datum they are invoked to explain confer no support at all.

### H-G: goodness

<!-- id: c12-p014 -->
Rowe, Adams, Sutherland, Schellenberg and Howard-Snyder are inspected selected attributions, and the 1996 hiddenness exchange and the 2025 précis are not a direct reply sequence. The inferences are evidential and attribute-sensitive. Suffering and nonresistant nonbelief challenge a specified package of power, knowledge and perfect love, if the evidential and normative premises hold. The restoration and delay defenses assume goodness and restoration rather than establishing them. And unrestricted appeals to unknown reasons weaken the positive recognition of goodness at the same rate as the objections. The assessment is that the rich perfect-governance package faces substantive conditional challenges while bare goodness remains unresolved. Restorative coherence addresses a victim's final loss only if actual same-person restoration occurs, and it does not alone justify permitting the harm. The boundary is that declared purposes can be indifferent or harmful, that the exact goodness and love commitments must be tested, and that compensation does not by itself justify permission. What would change the assessment is a benevolent specification that restricted in advance what counts as an expected world and survived the test; a specification that predicts nothing is not strengthened by the record.

### H-A: moral authority

<!-- id: c12-p015 -->
The author attributions and the descriptive-versus-normative distinctions are reliable, and the constructivist and robust-realist originals inspected in Phase 2 have narrowed, without closing, the gap in rival coverage. The inference is that the authority relation partly specifies its own target: shared subjecthood, creator status, sanctions and power do not supply it, and the availability of rival explanations challenges uniqueness rather than logically excluding every conceivable conscious authority. The assessment is that a distinct normative warrant is required and that unique conscious grounding is unsupported. Conscious subjects make benefit and harm possible, but value, impartiality and authority require normative premises; Craig supplies a conditional nature-and-command account, Railton a developed naturalist rival, Korsgaard a constructivist bridge with its own declared remainder, and Enoch a realism whose foundational debt its own author prices. The boundary is that consciousness, unity, creation, power and endurance do not automatically supply binding reasons, and that missing primary rival defenders prohibit broad elimination claims. What would change the assessment is a defended normative relation between the foundation's nature and legitimate claims on agents, assessed against the strongest rival originals rather than reconstructions of them.

<!-- id: c12-p016 -->
Four cross-cutting targets sit above the components, and they are conditional synthesis targets, not four more observations. The explanation of concrete existence and lawfulness remains a conditional conscious-ground proposal whose stopping point must be priced symmetrically with every rival's. Enduring personal significance remains a conditional enhancement: worthwhile continuation could add goods, contingent on actual survival, identity and value, while endless duration is neither sufficient for value nor shown necessary for all meaning. Finite significance is supported under an explicit value premise, and it leaves the question of enduring cosmic significance exactly where the person who asked it will find it, unanswered. And the integrated package retains every one of its separate connecting premises; the four circularity rules of the cumulative chapter govern any attempt to spend one of them twice.

## What would change these conclusions

<!-- id: c12-p017 -->
Suppose someone wished to overturn the empirical half of this thesis. What would they need? Not another retelling, and not another critic's reply, however welcome both would be as documentation. They would need one episode in which an independently recorded report is connected to a prespecified target exposure, to the individual's physiology, and to a narrowly defined acquisition interval, with the ordinary routes of information assessed. That is the conjunction the empirical chapter found in no inspected case. It is a demanding standard, and it is meant to be, because the celebrated cases show exactly what happens without it: single reporting chains whose decisive measurements were never made or never kept. The same standard binds the countercase. A critic who wants to show that a particular report had an ordinary cause needs case-specific grounds for that mechanism, not the name of a possibility.

<!-- id: c12-p018 -->
Suppose instead someone wished to advance the metaphysical routes. The comparison identifies what each of them still owes. A constrained rule for grounding and individuation, which could fail under neural and perceptual contrasts, would give the universal proposal the discriminating power it now lacks. A defended modal bridge, or a defended necessary-ground bridge at full modal strength, would convert one of the fundamentality routes from a conditional argument into an argument whose premises had been earned. And a comparison conducted against directly inspected rival originals, rather than against reconstructions of missing ones, would lift the restriction that now withholds every family ranking. Several of those originals are named in the gap register, and a bounded repair packet is the project's instrument for obtaining them.

<!-- id: c12-p019 -->
For survival and timelessness the requirement is severe and should not be softened. Survival needs actual further same-person subjectivity together with a defensible identity criterion; the coherence of a model, the eternal representation of a life, the persistence of a record and the value of a successor do not meet it, singly or together. Timelessness needs an account of a non-successive bearer and its relation to temporal contents that can answer the presence objection, not a report of altered duration, and not a physical formalism that omits a privileged time parameter while retaining earlier-and-later relations. These are present evidence limits. They are not proofs of impossibility, and this thesis has been careful never to convert one into the other.

<!-- id: c12-p020 -->
For design, goodness and authority, the requirement is discipline before evidence. A design argument earns support only through a defensible account of what the candidate agent would be inclined to produce, stated apart from the datum it is invoked to explain, with the alternatives in play and the assumptions governing their expectations declared. A goodness defense must distinguish an actual restoration from a coherent one, and a justified permission from a compensation. An account of authority must supply the normative relation itself, and not merely more consciousness, more unity or more power. In each case the record contains the arguments that show where the burden lies. It does not contain their discharge.

<!-- id: c12-p021 -->
And what would strengthen the case for the proposal by weakening the countercase? Symmetry demands the question. A transmission or interface model that fixed its constraints independently and then predicted which interventions would change which contents, rather than accommodating each after the fact, would convert bare compatibility with the stimulation evidence into an explanatory achievement. A theory-level test that perturbed candidate regions and contrasted conscious with unconscious processing, which the largest test in the record did not do, would sharpen what neural dependence establishes. And a completed illusionist or identity account that preserved the explanandum would settle a dispute this thesis has left open in both directions. None of these would establish brain independence; each would change what the countercase could claim.

## Open questions

<!-- id: c12-p022 -->
The discriminating needs can be ranked, and the synthesis stage ranked them. First, episode-linked acquisition and exposure controls, because they are the only route by which the crisis evidence could ever do the work its advocates want from it. Second, an independently warranted identity relation and evidence of beyond-death subjectivity, because without them no anomaly, however striking, reaches the survival component. Third, constrained grounding, individuation and brain-mapping rules, because without them the universal proposal cannot be distinguished from its plural and physical rivals. Fourth, defended candidate preferences and selection assumptions for the design comparison. Fifth, normative premises for authority and permission, assessed against stronger original rival coverage than the record now contains. Records that are inaccessible remain gaps. They are not invented requests, and they are not findings.

<!-- id: c12-p023 -->
The literature repairs still owed are listed by name in the disposition register, and the reader should understand why they are claim-triggered rather than active. The project's rule is that a stronger intended conclusion must be mapped to the gap that blocks it, and that the gap must be repaired under review before the conclusion is drawn. No chapter of this thesis drew such a conclusion, so no packet was triggered. That is a fact about restraint, not about the unimportance of the missing texts. The missing texts are of two kinds, and the difference matters. The exact 2006 Strawson article, a Kastrup reply to the selected objection, the developed indispensability argument at Enoch's missing pages, the 2013 Metz chapter and the 1990 Adams print would each clarify a debate, and none of them would supply a measurement. The earliest Neal and Alexander accounts, the Sabom chapter and the remaining dentures documents are different. They are public documents that bear on first-report chronology and on who attributed what to whom, and in the Sabom chapter's case on which measurements were reported; the register treats them as decision-relevant for exactly that reason. What they cannot do is manufacture the private charts, traces and independently timed exclusions that were never made or never kept.

<!-- id: c12-p024 -->
One open question deserves to be stated in the terms of the person who asked it, because the method was designed to preserve that concern rather than replace it. The question was whether embodied lives have enduring significance through a conscious foundation not confined to physical succession. Finite benefit is supported under an explicit value premise: on the finite-value argument, a benefit within a bounded interval can ground significance, and later absence alone does not erase it. That argument is a project-authored conditional whose inference strength the record marks as contested, so it stands here as a labeled conditional and not as a finding of the record. Granted its premise, it removes one reason for treating cosmology as a prerequisite of ordinary significance. But it does not answer the question asked. Enduring personal and cosmic significance would require actual same-person continuation in worthwhile relationships under a purposive and good foundation, and each of those conditions remains, on the evidence reviewed here, a conditional. The honest state of the question is open. Nothing in this thesis closes it in either direction.

<!-- id: c12-p025 -->
Procedurally, the thesis is a working draft until its own gates close. The coverage gate must accept the disposition register in Appendix C and allocate the transferred review budget. The whole-thesis adversarial review must check integration, circular bridges, double counting and the executed sensitivity results as one document. The release adjudication must then decide, with recorded justification, whether the two review layers jointly satisfy the final-review contract that the article never received. Only then is a versioned thesis released, with its exact deltas from the article and the frozen version 1, and only then do the pending Phase 2 review and gate close. Nothing in this chapter anticipates those decisions.

<!-- id: c12-p026 -->
Let me end where the article ends, because ten chapters have earned its last sentence. The defensible ambition is a constrained unification whose connections earn support together, not a metaphysical vocabulary in which the desired consequences are already contained. Such a programme remains open. Its success would consist in explaining more while risking more: preserving the experiential target, recovering the neural constraints, individuating actual subjects, and making intelligible predictions about a world governed by its proposed values. This thesis has not shown that the programme succeeds, and it has not shown that it fails. It has shown what success would require, component by component. What the evidence currently in hand supplies is narrower than any of those requirements, and it should be stated in the record's own terms. On the empirical side there is positive evidence of embodied neural contribution in the tested stimulation contexts. That is a constraint every surviving model must recover; it does not by itself settle brain independence, identity or a complete neural explanation, and it does not negate every independence proposal. On the value side there is finite significance, supported under an explicit and contested value premise, which is an argument and not a measurement. Nothing more ambitious is supplied. All twelve withheld stronger-conclusion gates remain withheld. That is the result, and it is stated as one.
