<!-- id: c02-p001 -->
The method of this thesis is critical comparative synthesis of selected philosophical exchanges and empirical studies. It is comparative because no position under examination can be evaluated in isolation: the question is never whether a view has difficulties, since every sufficiently ambitious view does, but whether its difficulties are worse than those of its developed rivals. It is critical because the comparison is conducted against explicit criteria rather than by accumulated impression. And it is a synthesis of selected material rather than an exhaustive survey: the project's searches, inclusions and inspections were bounded by prespecified budgets, and the boundaries themselves are part of the record. This chapter sets out the criteria, the evidence taxonomy, the search and inclusion protocol, the review and acceptance workflow, the dependence controls, and the sensitivity discipline that together govern every substantive claim in the chapters that follow. It closes by stating what this method cannot deliver, because the honest reach of the conclusions depends on those refusals.

<!-- id: c02-p002 -->
The method was not improvised during drafting, but neither did it precede all of the work it governs. A prespecified evidence and search protocol was proposed as a project task, independently reviewed, and frozen with a recorded hash as `work/G0/protocol_v1.md`; the evidence tasks that followed were bound by that frozen file and, after the pilot gate, by its numbered successor, which inherits the exact frozen text together with seven recorded procedural repairs. The freeze itself carries a recorded bootstrap exception: the three opening tasks that established the project's definitions, comparison families and this protocol predate the freeze; the opening comparison task's omitted backward- and forward-citation passes are expressly not counted as executed; and the six comparison families were not thereby source-verified as representative of the literature. Later phases audited coverage against the protocol and recorded ten binding synthesis constraints, nine component boundaries and eight prespecified sensitivity tests before any cumulative assessment was drafted; these are standing constraints on synthesis, not certification that the protocol was followed without remainder. Where execution fell short of a protocol rule, the shortfall is recorded in the gates and registers rather than silently absorbed. Method statements in this chapter trace to the frozen protocol, the project execution framework and the recorded synthesis constraints, and are mapped to those documents in the thesis crosswalk as labeled method premises rather than as evidence claims.

## Comparative criteria and the role of primitives

<!-- id: c02-p003 -->
Arguments and explanatory proposals are assessed against five criteria. Explanatory adequacy asks whether the proposal actually addresses the problem it claims to solve, rather than redescribing it or changing the subject. Coherence asks whether the proposal's commitments can be held together without internal contradiction or ad hoc partitioning. Independent motivation asks whether the machinery a proposal invokes is supported by considerations other than the problem it was introduced to solve. Fit with the evidence asks how the proposal stands to the observations and reports actually in the project record, including the unfavorable ones. Discriminatory power asks whether the proposal generates expectations that differ from those of its rivals, since a view compatible with every possible observation gains nothing from any particular observation. The comparison annex incorporated into the frozen protocol operationalizes essentially these criteria as six labeled checks, the sixth of which, remaining problems, records what a position still cannot explain after its best defense; in this chapter's terms that sixth check is carried by the strongest-reply-and-remaining-cost rule described below.

<!-- id: c02-p004 -->
Discriminatory power carries a further discipline. A theory's ability to accommodate a result after the fact is tracked separately from a successful prior prediction, because nearly any developed metaphysical framework can absorb nearly any single observation once the observation is known. Where a chapter credits a position with empirical fit, it states whether the fit was predicted or accommodated. The comparison also requires each favored explanation to specify what would weaken it as well as what would strengthen it; an assessment that can only be strengthened is not an assessment.

<!-- id: c02-p005 -->
A stopping point is not objectionable simply because it is primitive. Every sufficiently ambitious theory encounters basic commitments: the physicalist takes fundamental physical properties and laws as given, the theist takes a necessary being as given, the panpsychist takes proto-experiential properties as given. The relevant questions about any proposed primitive are three. Does it address the problem for which it was introduced, in the sense that positing it genuinely discharges the explanatory demand rather than renaming it? Does it constrain subsequent explanation, so that adopting it has consequences that could in principle embarrass it? And does it impose costs comparable to those accepted in rivals, or does it purchase its advantages by tolerating what no rival would be permitted to tolerate? These questions, rather than any presumption against primitives as such, do the comparative work throughout the thesis.

<!-- id: c02-p006 -->
The comparison spans six families of positions: physicalist, psychophysical-law, panpsychist, dualist, idealist and theistic. Each family receives its strongest explicit formulation available in the project record, not a convenient weak representative; whether that strongest available formulation is an inspected original defender or a weaker stand-in is a separately recorded fact, and its consequences for what may be concluded are stated at the end of this chapter. Family membership settles less than is often assumed: the project records, for each declared model, which commitments are required, which are excluded and which remain open, and these classifications concern the content of a model rather than anyone's confidence in it. In particular, moral and identity positions are not inferred from membership in a metaphysical family; a physicalist is not assigned moral antirealism, and a theist is not assigned a particular theory of personal identity, unless the declared model actually includes those commitments.

<!-- id: c02-p007 -->
The object of assessment is likewise disaggregated. The thesis evaluates nine hypothesis components separately: the fundamentality of consciousness, a universal conscious whole, independence of experience from ordinary brain function, personal survival of death, timelessness, preservation, intention, foundational goodness and moral authority. Evidence bearing on one component cannot silently transfer to another. The recorded component boundaries make this concrete: irreducibility of experience or a necessary ground does not by itself establish one universal subject; influence of the brain on experience is not the negation of every independence proposal; fidelity of a preserved record is not personal survival; production of a cosmos is not intended production; and consciousness, unity, creation, power and endurance do not automatically supply binding moral reasons. Each finding chapter names the components its material bears on and respects these boundaries when stating what has and has not been supported.

## Evidence taxonomy and the report/event distinction

<!-- id: c02-p008 -->
The protocol requires every claim to state its epistemic type, drawn from six categories: reported experience, observed event, documented measurement, philosophical premise, normative premise or inference. The type governs what the claim can support. A reported experience establishes, at most, that a person gave an account with a particular content at a particular time; an observed event requires corroboration beyond the report itself; a documented measurement carries its instrument, timing and context with it; a philosophical premise is adopted, not discovered; a normative premise is likewise adopted and separately flagged; and an inference is only as strong as the weakest step it rests on. In practice the ledger type fields are free text and have drifted well beyond these six labels; the taxonomy binds as a recording rule and a review criterion, not as an enforced schema, and the drift is itself a recorded discipline gap. The taxonomy exists to make each collapse of one type into another visible as a specific recordable error.

<!-- id: c02-p009 -->
Three distinctions guide the empirical comparison. First, a report is distinguished from its reported event: verifying that an account was given, published on a particular date and preserved accurately is not verifying that the events described occurred. Second, an observation is distinguished from its interpretation: a measured correlation between neural intervention and altered experience is an observation, while the claim that experience is identical to or constituted by neural activity is an interpretation that requires separate argument. Third, an observed dependence is distinguished from an identity claim: however systematically experience depends on brain function, dependence and identity are different relations, and evidence for the former is not automatically evidence for the latter. These distinctions are required by the protocol and checked in review; they are commitments with recorded enforcement points, not merely announcements, though the enforcement runs through review judgment rather than through any closed schema.

<!-- id: c02-p010 -->
Case evidence receives a chronological decomposition. For each case the record separates the medical events and measurements, the inferred timing of the reported experience, the first available account, later corroboration, the subject's ordinary information access, and subsequent revisions of the account. Uncertainty intervals and contradictions are retained rather than smoothed. Three further claims are kept distinct because they are routinely conflated: that information was not in circulation around a subject, that relevant brain activity was not measured, and that relevant brain activity was demonstrably absent. Absence of measurement is never treated as demonstrated absence of processing, and demonstrated absence in a monitored channel is never extended to channels that were not monitored.

<!-- id: c02-p011 -->
Explanatory proposals about particular cases observe a parallel restraint. A possible physiological mechanism is not an established explanation of a specific episode; naming hypoxia or anesthetic action explains a given report only if the proposed mechanism fits that episode's documented timing and circumstances. Symmetrically, the credentials and sincerity of a witness do not certify the accuracy of the account, and a financial interest in a story does not establish that it is fabricated. Each of these considerations enters the record as a provenance fact with a bounded evidential role, not as a verdict.

<!-- id: c02-p012 -->
Philosophical arguments are recorded with their premises, bridge principles and conclusions separated, and each objection is classified by its target: validity, premise truth, empirical adequacy, explanatory scope or normative grounding. The strongest available reply is recorded together with its remaining cost, since a reply that succeeds by abandoning the position's original advantage has not left the dialectic where it found it. Two specific inferences are barred by standing rule because they recur in this literature: explanatory dissatisfaction is not treated as proof of impossibility, and coherence is not treated as evidence of actuality. Two further boundary rules govern the components most exposed to overreach: subjective timelessness reported in experience does not itself establish ontological timelessness, and information preserved in any substrate, including a proposed universal mind, does not itself establish a continuing personal subject.

<!-- id: c02-p013 -->
Every decisive claim and argument carries a counterevidence entry: an actual opposing observation or argument, an alternative explanation with distinguishing expectations, or a documented unsuccessful search accompanied by an unresolved-coverage note. The requirement is for genuine opposition, not manufactured balance; no quota of objections is invented where the record contains none, and a documented failure to find counterevidence is recorded as exactly that, with its search preserved, rather than as positive support.

## Search, inclusion and the frozen literature cutoff

<!-- id: c02-p014 -->
Evidence gathering was staged. The project began with two deliberately narrow pilots, a single named near-death case dossier and a single philosophical argument exchange, each independently reviewed, with the explicit purpose of testing retrieval, extraction and inference boundaries before expansion. Method defects found in the pilots were recorded and repaired at a gate before broader assignments were issued; the repairs closed procedural defects, not the pilots' evidence gaps, several of which remain open in the project's register. Subsequent tasks proceeded under bounded contracts covering prospective studies, additional cases, historical and cross-cultural reports, comparative physiological states and the remaining philosophical domains, with a later gate auditing the accumulated coverage before any synthesis. The pilots supply no population estimate and no representative sample, and the staged structure is a workflow fact rather than an evidential one: as of 13 September 2026, forty-one of the forty-six Phase 2 assignments stood accepted, and those are forty-one completed procedures, not forty-one independent observations.

<!-- id: c02-p015 -->
Searches followed a prespecified grid of query families spanning named cases and their documented challenges, prospective studies and their null results, retrospective and cross-cultural collections, distressing and conflicting reports, comparative physiological states, the qualia and emergence literature, neural intervention evidence, existence and fine-tuning arguments, time and identity, meaning and purpose, and moral foundations. Each contract selected its rows and prespecified its query allocation before retrieval. Two balance rules applied throughout: searches used challenging as well as neutral and favorable terms, and named-case tasks reserved queries for documentation, for serious challenge and for reply or alternative account, so that no case entered the record accompanied only by its friendly retellings. A stratum for which no source could be included is represented by its executed search and an explicit gap, never by a substitute presented as representative.

<!-- id: c02-p016 -->
Screening was bounded and recorded. The default window was the first twenty results actually exposed by an engine for each executed query, or all exposed results if fewer, screened identically for favorable and unfavorable queries, with ranking and visible identifiers preserved and truncation risks marked. Ranked search results are not a random sample, and no claim in the thesis treats them as one. For each included original source the protocol requires one backward-reference pass and one forward-citation search where accessible within budget, with candidates and selection reasons logged. That requirement was not met everywhere: the frozen protocol itself records that the opening comparison task's omitted passes are expressly not counted as executed, and later gate records note further incomplete passes. The recorded synthesis constraints add the standing limit that bounded title queries are discovery, never a complete cited-by index. Discovery of a reference is in any case not inspection of it: a source found in another paper's bibliography remains an unverified lead until its relevant text is actually examined.

<!-- id: c02-p017 -->
The audit trail runs from query to conclusion. Every executed search has a logged record with date, destination actually used, exact query string, filters, candidates, inclusions, exclusions and access limits, and the destination logged is the one actually queried; a web index of a database's pages is not recorded as a search of that database. Candidate sources may receive identifiers while remaining unverified leads, and uninspected candidates are never counted as inspected sources. Conversation statements and inherited references likewise start as unverified leads regardless of their apparent authority.

<!-- id: c02-p018 -->
Material was included if it addressed a contracted question and provided an original empirical report, study methods and results, a relevant primary historical account, an explicit philosophical argument, or a substantive criticism or reply. Null, unfavorable, distressing and conflicting results were included under identical relevance criteria, a requirement that matters in a literature whose popular stratum is dominated by favorable narratives. Firsthand narrative evidence was retained despite documentation gaps, labeled by what it can establish. Secondary accounts, interviews and surveys could orient a search, locate originals or establish that a statement was made; they do not substitute for missing original records.

<!-- id: c02-p019 -->
Material was excluded as independent evidence only for being an exact duplicate, a retelling with no new observation, irrelevant to the contracted question, or inaccessible in its substantive claims. For the last category the lead and the access gap are preserved, because exclusion for inaccessibility is not a finding that the claim is false. Nothing was excluded for religious affiliation, advocacy, author credentials or commercial motive alone; such facts were recorded as provenance considerations bearing on weight, not as gates on admission.

<!-- id: c02-p020 -->
Inspection standards were set by what a claim needs. Decisive claims prefer original studies and authors' own arguments, inspected at the exact passage with page, section, table or paragraph locators, together with the methods, results and limitations required to interpret the passage. An abstract or snippet is insufficient for any claim resting on unseen denominators or methods. Partial access is labeled precisely, and secondhand inspection is never upgraded: when an author quotes a medical chart, the record says that the quotation was inspected, not the chart. Quotation is kept within applicable limits, with paraphrase and precise locators preferred.

<!-- id: c02-p021 -->
The literature base is frozen by publication date, not by a stop-clock on searching. The corpus comprises the earliest retrievable publications through 11 September 2026 inclusive, with no lower publication-date bound; event date, report date, publication date and retrieval date are recorded separately for each source, and searches themselves carry their actual execution dates in the logs. Material published after the cutoff is logged as an update lead rather than silently mixed into the frozen corpus, and any later extension of the cutoff is a prospective, separately logged decision of a task contract. The language scope is English full texts and accessible English translations, with original language and translation provenance recorded; unresolved translation access is a coverage gap, not evidence that no relevant literature exists, and the English constraint is itself a recorded bias on cultural generalizations. Under these constraints the project is a bounded review with systematic procedures. It is not, and does not describe itself as, an exhaustive or completed systematic review.

<!-- id: c02-p022 -->
The method also fixes what was never authorized: no payments for access, no contact with witnesses or authors, no requests for private records, and no external messages in the course of evidence work. Where primary text was inaccessible by public routes, the attempted routes and the unresolved need were recorded and work continued elsewhere. Instructions embedded in source documents were treated as untrusted data throughout.

## Independent review and acceptance semantics

<!-- id: c02-p023 -->
The unit of work is a contracted task. An overseer issues a filled contract specifying the question, exclusions, inputs by exact hash, outputs, identifiers, budget and acceptance criteria; a researcher submits a packet containing the report, proposed ledger records and a result envelope; a reviewer who did not author the packet examines it; and the overseer adjudicates. Only the overseer writes the canonical ledgers, and proposed records enter them only after review. Every contract, packet and review identifies the protocol version and the exact hashes of its inputs, so that any later reader can determine precisely which version of which document a finding rests on.

<!-- id: c02-p024 -->
Review is inspection, not endorsement. The reviewer reopens every source supporting a decisive claim, checks the cited passages and the surrounding methods and context needed to interpret them, and samples the supporting material. Review verifies query coverage against the contract, selection and exclusion reasons, accessibility labels, denominator units, duplicate and overlap handling, chronology, the strongest alternatives, counterevidence entries, bridge premises and the separation of the three confidence judgments described below. Reviews operate under their own budgets, with a default cap and scoped extensions when decisive sources exceed it; a decisive source is never silently skipped for budget reasons.

<!-- id: c02-p025 -->
The verdicts have fixed semantics. Accept means the contracted method was met, explicitly including the case where the substantive result is an honestly unresolved question; acceptance is a statement about procedure, never about metaphysical truth. Revise means a repairable material defect with bounded corrections. Unresolved means decisive evidence is inaccessible or a substantive disagreement persists, and the review states which conclusion is limited as a result. Four defects block unconditional acceptance outright: a decisive citation error, a concealed missing denominator, an overcount of independence, and an unmarked inference from report to event. The chapters of this thesis inherit these adjudicated statuses rather than improving on them.

<!-- id: c02-p026 -->
At most two substantive revision cycles are permitted per task, after which the disagreement is recorded or the task is split, so that iteration cannot substitute for resolution. No author approves their own output, reviewers report directly to the overseer rather than to authors, and disagreements are preserved in the record rather than reconciled by fiat. Agreement among the models used to execute tasks supplies no additional empirical observation: two systems endorsing the same claim is one claim, and the project draws no support from unanimity as such.

<!-- id: c02-p027 -->
Findings are versioned. Method changes create a numbered protocol version with a recorded decision stating what changed, why, and which prior tasks require reassessment. When an accepted upstream finding changes, affected downstream tasks are marked stale or blocked pending review rather than silently retaining conclusions built on the earlier version. Submitted and reviewed versions of every packet are preserved, and new original data or repaired claims require fresh distinct-author review rather than inheriting the acceptance of what they replace.

<!-- id: c02-p028 -->
These acceptance semantics bound what the thesis can claim for itself. Its findings are accepted within a recorded project procedure by AI-assisted authors and reviewers under an overseer; they have not undergone journal peer review, and nothing in this document converts procedural acceptance into external validation. Where a chapter reports that a claim is supported, the term names the project's recorded judgment with its recorded scope, not a fact about the consensus of any research community.

## Dependence tracking and double-counting controls

<!-- id: c02-p029 -->
Sources are deduplicated by stable identifier first, then by normalized title, authors, year and content. Preprints, published versions, corrections and supplements are linked, and versions are distinguished wherever a relevant assertion changes between them, because an argument quietly strengthened between editions is a different argument. The same article hosted at different locations is one source with several access routes, not several sources.

<!-- id: c02-p030 -->
Cases are deduplicated by the underlying person, event, date, site and chronology. Books, interviews, sermons and copied stories about one episode share a duplicate group and count once; a widely retold case is one case however large its literature. Distinct episodes involving one person are recorded as related rather than assumed independent. Apparent corroboration receives the same scrutiny: a new witness is tagged with relationship, information exposure and statement timing, and several witnesses repeating one briefing are not independent corroboration but one account with several voices.

<!-- id: c02-p031 -->
Studies and samples are checked for overlap by recruitment sites, enrollment dates, registry identifiers, participant counts and authors' own overlap statements, with secondary analyses, pooled studies and follow-ups linked to their underlying cohorts. Sample sizes are never summed where disjointness is unestablished. Dependence risks are tracked even where participants differ: shared recruitment channels and cultural transmission of narrative motifs can make formally distinct collections evidentially entangled. The same discipline extends to philosophy, where arguments sharing a load-bearing premise are assigned to a dependency group, so that the cumulative chapter cannot count one premise's plausibility several times under several argument names.

<!-- id: c02-p032 -->
Rates and counts keep their anatomy. Every empirical rate preserves its numerator, denominator, observation unit, eligibility definition, timeframe, exclusions and missingness, with an exact locator; an unknown is never replaced with zero, and a missing target opportunity is never scored as a failed test. Prospective studies are extracted along their full flow, from eligible events through enrollment, survival, interview eligibility, consent, completed interviews, classified experiences, monitored cases and target exposure to identification attempts, and categories that a study does not report as nested are not forced into a single attrition chain. Proportions are computed only from compatible units over justified denominators, study-specific rates are retained before any aggregation, and pooling requires a separately justified contract.

<!-- id: c02-p033 -->
Double-counting is finally controlled at the level of use. A single claim may bear on several hypothesis components, but it remains one observation however many components it touches. Within this thesis the control is structural: every substantive paragraph is mapped in a crosswalk to the specific claim, argument, case and source records or labeled premises it rests on, so that reuse of one record across chapters is visible and checkable rather than diffused through prose. The cumulative chapter's dependence map draws on these crosswalks, and the whole-thesis review checks them for exactly this failure.

## Sensitivity testing and quantitative restraint

<!-- id: c02-p034 -->
Three assessments are kept separate for every claim and never averaged. Record reliability asks whether this exact observation, report, quotation or attribution is documented with usable provenance, and states whether the judgment applies to the existence of a report or to its described event. Inference strength asks how well a particular conclusion follows from the record against serious alternatives, with bridge premises, dependence and decisive missing information stated. Component confidence asks what provisional assessment each of the nine hypothesis components warrants given the reviewed evidence and objections. Each assessment uses the qualitative labels supported, contested, unresolved or challenged, with reasons and scope; the labels are not numerical bins, and no arithmetic over them is defined or used.

<!-- id: c02-p035 -->
The separations do real work. A reliable narrative may support only a weak metaphysical inference: that an account is well documented, early, stable and sincere settles nothing about whether its best explanation is exotic. An accurate attribution of a philosophical premise to its author is not evidence that the premise is true. And a claim's reliability label is never inherited as confidence in a hypothesis component; the inference from record to component always passes through stated bridge premises that can themselves be contested, and a task-level unresolved label is not converted into a cumulative prior or into equal support for competing families.

<!-- id: c02-p036 -->
Numerical probabilities are permitted but disciplined. They require explicitly specified hypotheses, defensible prior and likelihood ranges, stated dependence assumptions and sensitivity analysis. Overlapping metaphysical families are not forced to sum to one hundred percent, because the families are not exclusive or exhaustive in the required way. Where defensible likelihoods are unavailable, and in this evidence base they generally are, the thesis provides conditional qualitative comparisons and states plainly that the evidence does not identify a numerical posterior. No pooled posterior over cases or samples appears anywhere in this document, because the denominators, disjointness and likelihoods that such a number would require were not established.

<!-- id: c02-p037 -->
Robustness is tested by withdrawal rather than by weighting. The article's qualitative rule, remove particular cases or premises and ask which reasons remain, is executed in the cumulative chapter as eight prespecified sensitivity tests: removal of the strongest disputed named near-death case, selected by explicit criterion rather than convenience, with a second candidate shown wherever the strength ordering is ambiguous; omission of all retrospective cases and narratives, including the retrospective community arm, while retaining truly prospective observations at their real denominators; removal of contributions whose sample or event dependence is uncertain; variation of personal-identity criteria across psychological, organismic and simple-subject views, of strict identity against prudential continuity, and of the four recorded preservation models; removal of the central endurance-required-for-value, unity-to-obligation and consciousness-to-authority bridge premises; removal of the global sufficient-reason, epistemic-to-metaphysical and life-valuing designer-preference premises one at a time; removal of the strongest selected neural-intervention constraint or report-to-experience proxy, with the independence model's actual predictive constraints retained, so that the countercase is stress-tested symmetrically with the case; and restriction of the comparison to directly inspected positive original defenders and their direct replies, excluding project reconstructions from claims about authors. Each test reports its effect on each of the nine components separately.

<!-- id: c02-p038 -->
Stronger conclusions are gated rather than hedged. The recorded synthesis constraints require every stronger intended conclusion to be mapped to the specific gap that blocks it, and the project's register records thirty-five gap groups, each with an explicit closure condition, together with twelve withheld stronger-conclusion gates, all currently withheld. Before any chapter of this thesis asserts one of those conclusions, the blocking gap must have been repaired under review or the conclusion is explicitly withheld with its gate cited; generic caveats do not license a gated verdict. Appendix C carries the full disposition register, and the concluding chapters state which gates remain closed and what that costs the argument.

## What this method cannot deliver

<!-- id: c02-p039 -->
The method's coverage is bounded and asymmetric. Searches were budgeted, the screening windows were finite, the corpus is frozen at the publication cutoff, and the language scope skews cultural coverage toward English-accessible material. Several bounds are sharper than a reader of the protocol's requirements alone would infer. The destinations named in the search grid were planned venues, not claims of database access: where a database was unavailable, the logs record a public web index queried in its place, and the two are not equated. Engine rankings are dynamic and cannot be reproduced exactly; the logs preserve what was visible, not a rerunnable experiment. And the backward- and forward-citation obligation was not executed everywhere, beginning with the recorded opening exception, so the citation network around the included sources is bounded discovery rather than a completed map. Not every position is represented by an inspected original defender: the record distinguishes inspected originals, secondary attributions, project reconstructions and uninspected variants, and comparative verdicts are restricted accordingly. In particular, no broad ranking of worldview families is asserted where a rival's strongest formulation was never directly inspected; conclusions of that shape are either narrowed to the formulations actually examined or withheld under the register described above.

<!-- id: c02-p041 -->
The enforcement and review layers have their own honest limits. The closed taxonomies this chapter describes, six epistemic types and four assessment labels, bind as rules and review criteria, but the ledger fields that carry them are free text and have drifted into a much larger vocabulary; discipline lives in review judgment, not in a schema that rejects a mislabeled record. The independent review this chapter describes is review by a distinct AI model context under the same overseer, protocol and toolchain as the author; it is not re-inspection by human specialists, and it does not include access to medical charts or private records that no route in this project could authorize. The contracted independent final review of the article that this thesis expands remains unperformed; its obligations transfer, without weakening, into this thesis's chapter reviews and whole-thesis review, and until that closure the article's own conclusions hold the corresponding provisional status. The eight sensitivity tests were specified in full at the synthesis audit, after most evidence gathering: they are prespecified relative to the cumulative assessment they constrain, not relative to evidence collection. Finally, the public snapshot of this project excludes downloaded source files and raw audit materials, so a public reader can verify the record structure and the reasoning but cannot replay the inspections themselves.

<!-- id: c02-p040 -->
The method also cannot convert its own rigor into truth. Procedural acceptance, however disciplined, is a fact about this project's workflow; the requirements that a distinct reviewer reopen decisive sources and that disagreements be recorded raise the cost of error without abolishing it. The assessments that follow are qualitative, comparative and conditional. They identify no numerical posterior, certify no completed systematic review, and claim no external peer validation. What the discipline of this chapter establishes is a requirement, not an achievement: every substantive conclusion in the following chapters must be traceable through the crosswalk to identified records and labeled premises, testable by the stated withdrawals, and checkable against the stated gates, so that a reader who rejects a conclusion can locate exactly which record, premise or bridge they are rejecting. Whether each chapter meets that requirement is decided by its own independent review, not by this chapter's description of the rule.
