<!-- id: c02-p001 -->
The method of this thesis is critical comparative synthesis of selected philosophical exchanges and empirical studies. Each word in that description earns its place. The method is comparative because no position under examination can be judged on its own. The question is never whether a view has difficulties, since every sufficiently ambitious view does. The question is whether its difficulties are worse than those of its developed rivals. It is critical because the comparison is conducted against explicit criteria, not by accumulated impression. And it is a synthesis of selected material, not an exhaustive survey. The project's searches, inclusions and inspections were bounded by budgets fixed in advance, and those boundaries are themselves part of the record. This chapter sets out the criteria, the evidence taxonomy, the search and inclusion protocol, the review and acceptance workflow, the dependence controls and the sensitivity discipline. Together they govern every substantive claim in the chapters that follow. The chapter closes by saying what this method cannot deliver, because the honest reach of the conclusions depends on those refusals.

<!-- id: c02-p002 -->
The method was not improvised during drafting. Neither, it must be said, did it come before all of the work it governs. A prespecified evidence and search protocol was proposed as a project task, independently reviewed, and frozen with a recorded hash as `work/G0/protocol_v1.md`. The evidence tasks that followed were bound by that frozen file and, after the pilot gate, by its numbered successor. The successor takes over the frozen text unchanged and adds seven recorded procedural repairs. The freeze also carries a recorded bootstrap exception, and it has three parts. The three opening tasks that established the project's definitions, its comparison families and this protocol came before the freeze. The backward- and forward-citation passes that the opening comparison task omitted are expressly not counted as executed. And the six comparison families were not thereby source-verified as representative of the literature. Later phases audited coverage against the protocol. Before any cumulative assessment was drafted, they recorded ten binding synthesis constraints, nine component boundaries and eight prespecified sensitivity tests. These are standing constraints on synthesis. They are not a certificate that the protocol was followed without remainder. Where execution fell short of a protocol rule, the shortfall is recorded in the gates and registers rather than quietly absorbed. The method statements in this chapter trace to the frozen protocol, the project execution framework and the recorded synthesis constraints. The thesis crosswalk maps them to those documents as labeled method premises, not as evidence claims.

## The criteria of comparison and the place of primitives

<!-- id: c02-p003 -->
Arguments and explanatory proposals are assessed against five criteria. Explanatory adequacy asks whether the proposal actually addresses the problem it claims to solve, rather than redescribing it or changing the subject. Coherence asks whether the proposal's commitments can be held together without internal contradiction and without ad hoc partitioning. Independent motivation asks whether the machinery a proposal invokes has support from considerations other than the problem it was brought in to solve. Fit with the evidence asks how the proposal stands to the observations and reports actually in the project record, the unfavorable ones included. Discriminatory power asks whether the proposal generates expectations that differ from those of its rivals. Why does that matter? Because a view compatible with every possible observation gains nothing from any particular observation. The comparison annex incorporated into the frozen protocol turns essentially these criteria into six labeled checks. The sixth, remaining problems, records what a position still cannot explain after its best defense. In this chapter's terms, that sixth check is carried by the rule about the strongest reply and its remaining cost, described below.

<!-- id: c02-p004 -->
Discriminatory power carries a further discipline. A theory's ability to accommodate a result after the fact is tracked separately from a successful prediction made in advance. The reason is familiar: nearly any developed metaphysical framework can absorb nearly any single observation once the observation is known. So whenever a chapter credits a position with empirical fit, it says whether the fit was predicted or accommodated. The comparison also requires each favored explanation to say what would weaken it, and not only what would strengthen it. An assessment that can only be strengthened is not an assessment.

<!-- id: c02-p005 -->
A stopping point is not objectionable simply because it is primitive. Every sufficiently ambitious theory has basic commitments somewhere. The physicalist takes fundamental physical properties and laws as given. The theist takes a necessary being as given. The panpsychist takes proto-experiential properties as given. The relevant questions about any proposed primitive are three. Does it address the problem it was introduced for, in the sense that positing it genuinely discharges the explanatory demand rather than renaming it? Does it constrain what comes after, so that adopting it has consequences that could in principle embarrass it? And does it impose costs comparable to those its rivals accept, or does it buy its advantages by tolerating what no rival would be allowed to tolerate? These three questions do the comparative work throughout the thesis. No presumption against primitives as such does any of it.

<!-- id: c02-p006 -->
The comparison spans six families of positions: physicalist, psychophysical-law, panpsychist, dualist, idealist and theistic. Each family receives the strongest explicit formulation available in the project record, not a convenient weak representative. Whether that strongest available formulation is an inspected original defender or a weaker stand-in is a separately recorded fact. What it means for what may be concluded is stated at the end of this chapter. Family membership settles less than is often assumed. For each declared model, the project records which commitments are required, which are excluded and which remain open, and these classifications concern the content of a model, not anyone's confidence in it. In particular, moral and identity positions are not inferred from membership in a metaphysical family. A physicalist is not assigned moral antirealism. A theist is not assigned a particular theory of personal identity. Neither is assigned anything of the kind unless the declared model actually includes the commitment.

<!-- id: c02-p007 -->
The object of assessment is taken apart in the same way. The thesis evaluates nine hypothesis components separately: the fundamentality of consciousness, a universal conscious whole, independence of experience from ordinary brain function, personal survival of death, timelessness, preservation, intention, foundational goodness and moral authority. Evidence bearing on one component cannot quietly transfer to another. The recorded component boundaries make this concrete. Irreducibility of experience, or a necessary ground, does not by itself establish one universal subject. Influence of the brain on experience is not the negation of every independence proposal. Fidelity of a preserved record is not personal survival. Production of a cosmos is not intended production. And consciousness, unity, creation, power and endurance do not automatically supply binding moral reasons. Each finding chapter names the components its material bears on, and it respects these boundaries when it says what has and has not been supported.

## The evidence taxonomy and the difference between a report and an event

<!-- id: c02-p008 -->
The protocol requires every claim to state its epistemic type, and there are six types to choose from: reported experience, observed event, documented measurement, philosophical premise, normative premise and inference. The type governs what the claim can support. A reported experience establishes, at most, that a person gave an account with a particular content at a particular time. An observed event requires corroboration beyond the report itself. A documented measurement carries its instrument, its timing and its context with it. A philosophical premise is adopted, not discovered. A normative premise is likewise adopted, and it is flagged separately. An inference is only as strong as the weakest step it rests on. There is, of course, a gap between the rule and the practice. The ledger's type fields are free text, and they have drifted well beyond these six labels. The taxonomy binds as a recording rule and a review criterion, not as an enforced schema, and the drift is itself a recorded discipline gap. Why keep the taxonomy at all, then? Because it makes each collapse of one type into another visible as a specific, recordable error.

<!-- id: c02-p009 -->
Three distinctions guide the empirical comparison. The first is between a report and the event it reports. To verify that an account was given, published on a particular date and preserved accurately is not to verify that the events it describes occurred. The second is between an observation and its interpretation. A measured correlation between a neural intervention and an altered experience is an observation. The claim that experience is identical to neural activity, or constituted by it, is an interpretation, and it needs its own argument. The third is between observed dependence and identity. However systematically experience depends on brain function, dependence and identity are different relations, and evidence for the first is not automatically evidence for the second. These distinctions are required by the protocol and checked in review. They are commitments with recorded points of enforcement, not mere announcements. It should be added that the enforcement runs through the judgment of reviewers rather than through any closed schema.

<!-- id: c02-p010 -->
Case evidence is broken down by time. For each case the record separates the medical events and measurements, the inferred timing of the reported experience, the first available account, the later corroboration, the subject's ordinary access to information, and the later revisions of the account. Intervals of uncertainty and outright contradictions are kept as they are, not smoothed away. Three further claims are kept apart because they are so often run together: that information was not in circulation around a subject, that relevant brain activity was not measured, and that relevant brain activity was demonstrably absent. These are three different things. Absence of measurement is never treated as demonstrated absence of processing. And demonstrated absence in a monitored channel is never extended to channels that were not monitored.

<!-- id: c02-p011 -->
Explanatory proposals about particular cases are held to a matching restraint. A possible physiological mechanism is not an established explanation of a specific episode. To name hypoxia or anesthetic action explains a given report only if the proposed mechanism fits that episode's documented timing and circumstances. The restraint cuts the other way too. The credentials and sincerity of a witness do not certify the accuracy of the account, and a financial interest in a story does not establish that the story was fabricated. Each of these considerations enters the record as a fact about provenance with a bounded evidential role. None of them enters as a verdict.

<!-- id: c02-p012 -->
Philosophical arguments are recorded with their premises, their bridge principles and their conclusions kept separate. Each objection is classified by what it targets: validity, premise truth, empirical adequacy, explanatory scope or normative grounding. The strongest available reply is recorded together with what it still costs. The reason is that a reply which succeeds by giving up the position's original advantage has not left the debate where it found it. Two particular inferences are barred by standing rule, because they recur in this literature. Explanatory dissatisfaction is not treated as proof of impossibility. Coherence is not treated as evidence of actuality. Two further boundary rules govern the components most exposed to overreach. Subjective timelessness reported in experience does not by itself establish ontological timelessness. And information preserved in any substrate, a proposed universal mind included, does not by itself establish a continuing personal subject.

<!-- id: c02-p013 -->
Every decisive claim and argument carries a counterevidence entry. The entry is one of three things: an actual opposing observation or argument, an alternative explanation with expectations that differ, or a documented unsuccessful search with a note that coverage remains unresolved. The requirement is for genuine opposition, not for manufactured balance. No quota of objections is invented where the record contains none. A documented failure to find counterevidence is recorded as just that, with its search preserved. It is not recorded as positive support.

## Search, inclusion and the frozen literature cutoff

<!-- id: c02-p014 -->
Evidence gathering was staged. The project began with two deliberately narrow pilots: a single named near-death case dossier and a single philosophical argument exchange. Each was independently reviewed, and the stated purpose of both was to test the limits of retrieval, extraction and inference before anything was expanded. Method defects found in the pilots were recorded and repaired at a gate before broader assignments went out. The repairs closed procedural defects. They did not close the pilots' evidence gaps, several of which remain open in the project's register. Later tasks proceeded under bounded contracts covering prospective studies, additional cases, historical and cross-cultural reports, comparative physiological states and the remaining philosophical domains, and a later gate audited the accumulated coverage before any synthesis. The pilots supply no population estimate and no representative sample. The staged structure is a fact about workflow, not a fact about evidence. As of 13 September 2026, forty-one of the forty-six Phase 2 assignments stood accepted. Those are forty-one completed procedures. They are not forty-one independent observations.

<!-- id: c02-p015 -->
Searches followed a grid of query families fixed in advance. The grid covered named cases and their documented challenges, prospective studies and their null results, retrospective and cross-cultural collections, distressing and conflicting reports, and comparative physiological states. It also covered the qualia and emergence literature, neural intervention evidence, existence and fine-tuning arguments, time and identity, meaning and purpose, and moral foundations. Each contract selected its rows and set its allocation of queries before retrieval began. Two balance rules applied throughout. Searches used challenging terms as well as neutral and favorable ones. And named-case tasks reserved queries for documentation, for serious challenge, and for reply or alternative account, so that no case entered the record accompanied only by its friendly retellings. Where no source could be included for a stratum, that stratum is represented by its executed search and an explicit gap. It is never represented by a substitute dressed up as representative.

<!-- id: c02-p016 -->
Screening was bounded and recorded. The default window was the first twenty results an engine actually exposed for each executed query, or all of the exposed results if there were fewer. Favorable and unfavorable queries were screened identically, ranking and visible identifiers were preserved, and risks of truncation were marked. Ranked search results are not a random sample, and no claim in the thesis treats them as one. For each included original source the protocol requires one backward-reference pass and one forward-citation search, where these are accessible within budget, with candidates and reasons for selection logged. That requirement was not met everywhere. The frozen protocol itself records that the passes omitted by the opening comparison task are expressly not counted as executed, and later gate records note further incomplete passes. The recorded synthesis constraints add a standing limit: bounded title queries are discovery, never a complete cited-by index. And discovering a reference is not inspecting it. A source found in another paper's bibliography remains an unverified lead until its relevant text has actually been examined.

<!-- id: c02-p017 -->
The audit trail is meant to run from query to conclusion, and the protocol's third rule says what that requires. Every executed search is to be logged with its date, the destination actually used, the query string as typed, the filters, the candidates, the inclusions, the exclusions and the limits on access. That is the rule. The record does not meet it everywhere, and the register says exactly where it fails. The search work of the earliest comparison task (T03) was lost. The two pilot batches were run mixed, so their per-query ranks cannot be recovered. And one combined forward query (the T05 OR query) was never captured. These are historical facts about the capture, and the register files them as a permanent audit loss: any replacement search carries a new date and repairs nothing about the old record. The rule binds everything else that was logged, and one of its particulars deserves emphasis. The destination logged is the one actually queried; a web index of a database's pages is not recorded as a search of that database. Candidate sources may receive identifiers while they remain unverified leads, and uninspected candidates are never counted as inspected sources. Statements made in conversation and inherited references likewise start as unverified leads, whatever their apparent authority.

<!-- id: c02-p018 -->
What was included? Material that addressed a contracted question and provided one of the following: an original empirical report, study methods and results, a relevant primary historical account, an explicit philosophical argument, or a substantive criticism or reply. Null, unfavorable, distressing and conflicting results were included under identical relevance criteria. That requirement matters in a literature whose popular stratum is dominated by favorable narratives. Firsthand narrative evidence was kept despite gaps in its documentation, and it was labeled by what it can establish. Secondary accounts, interviews and surveys could orient a search, locate originals, or establish that a statement was made. They do not stand in for missing original records.

<!-- id: c02-p019 -->
What was excluded? Material was excluded as independent evidence on only four grounds: it was an exact duplicate, it was a retelling with no new observation, it was irrelevant to the contracted question, or its substantive claims were inaccessible. In the last case the lead and the access gap are preserved, because exclusion for inaccessibility is not a finding that the claim is false. Nothing was excluded for religious affiliation, advocacy, author credentials or commercial motive alone. Such facts were recorded as considerations of provenance that bear on weight. They were not gates on admission.

<!-- id: c02-p020 -->
Standards of inspection were set by what a claim needs. For decisive claims the preference is for original studies and authors' own arguments, inspected at the very passage, with page, section, table or paragraph locators, and together with the methods, results and limitations needed to interpret that passage. An abstract or a snippet is not enough for any claim that rests on denominators or methods no one has seen. Partial access is labeled precisely, and secondhand inspection is never upgraded. When an author quotes a medical chart, the record says that the quotation was inspected, not the chart. Quotation is kept within the applicable limits, and paraphrase with precise locators is preferred.

<!-- id: c02-p021 -->
The literature base is frozen by publication date, not by a stop-clock on searching. The corpus runs from the earliest retrievable publications through 11 September 2026 inclusive, with no lower bound on publication date. For each source, the event date, the report date, the publication date and the retrieval date are recorded separately, and the searches themselves carry their actual execution dates in the logs. Material published after the cutoff is logged as an update lead; it is not quietly mixed into the frozen corpus. Any later extension of the cutoff is a prospective decision of a task contract, logged separately. The language scope is English full texts and accessible English translations, with the original language and the provenance of the translation recorded. Where access to a translation remains unresolved, that is a coverage gap, not evidence that no relevant literature exists, and the English constraint is itself a recorded bias on any cultural generalization. Under these constraints the project is a bounded review with systematic procedures. It is not an exhaustive or completed systematic review, and it does not describe itself as one.

<!-- id: c02-p022 -->
The method also fixes what was never authorized. There were no payments for access, no contact with witnesses or authors, no requests for private records, and no external messages in the course of evidence work. Where a primary text could not be reached by public routes, the routes attempted and the need left unmet were recorded, and the work continued elsewhere. Instructions embedded in source documents were treated as untrusted data throughout.

## Independent review and what acceptance means

<!-- id: c02-p023 -->
The unit of work is a contracted task, and each task passes through four hands. An overseer issues a filled contract that specifies the question, the exclusions, the inputs by exact hash, the outputs, the identifiers, the budget and the acceptance criteria. A researcher submits a packet containing the report, the proposed ledger records and a result envelope. A reviewer who did not write the packet examines it. And the overseer adjudicates. Only the overseer writes the canonical ledgers, and proposed records enter them only after review. Every contract, packet and review identifies the protocol version and the exact hashes of its inputs. A later reader can therefore determine precisely which version of which document a finding rests on.

<!-- id: c02-p024 -->
Review is inspection, not endorsement. The reviewer reopens every source that supports a decisive claim, checks the cited passages together with the surrounding methods and context needed to interpret them, and samples the supporting material. Review also verifies query coverage against the contract, the reasons for selection and exclusion, accessibility labels, denominator units, the handling of duplicates and overlap, and chronology. It verifies the strongest alternatives, the counterevidence entries, the bridge premises, and the separation of the three confidence judgments described below. Reviews run under their own budgets, with a default cap and scoped extensions when decisive sources exceed it. A decisive source is never quietly skipped to save budget.

<!-- id: c02-p025 -->
The verdicts have fixed meanings. Accept means that the contracted method was met, and this explicitly includes the case where the substantive result is an honestly unresolved question. Acceptance is a statement about procedure. It is never a statement about metaphysical truth. Revise means a material defect that can be repaired with bounded corrections. Unresolved means that decisive evidence is inaccessible or that a substantive disagreement persists, and the review says which conclusion is limited as a result. Four defects block unconditional acceptance outright: a decisive citation error, a concealed missing denominator, an overcount of independence, and an unmarked inference from report to event. The chapters of this thesis take these adjudicated statuses as they stand. They do not improve on them.

<!-- id: c02-p026 -->
At most two substantive revision cycles are permitted per task. After that, the disagreement is recorded or the task is split, so that iteration cannot substitute for resolution. No author approves their own output. Reviewers report directly to the overseer, not to authors. Disagreements are preserved in the record rather than reconciled by fiat. And agreement among the models used to execute tasks supplies no additional empirical observation. Two systems endorsing the same claim is one claim, and the project draws no support from unanimity as such.

<!-- id: c02-p027 -->
Findings are versioned. A change of method creates a numbered protocol version with a recorded decision that says what changed, why it changed, and which earlier tasks need reassessment. When an accepted upstream finding changes, the downstream tasks it affects are marked stale or blocked pending review. They do not quietly keep conclusions built on the earlier version. The submitted and the reviewed versions of every packet are preserved. New original data and repaired claims require fresh review by a distinct author; they do not take over the acceptance of what they replace.

<!-- id: c02-p028 -->
These meanings of acceptance bound what the thesis can claim for itself. Its findings are accepted within a recorded project procedure, by AI-assisted authors and reviewers under an overseer. They have not undergone journal peer review, and nothing in this document turns procedural acceptance into external validation. When a chapter reports that a claim is supported, the term names the project's recorded judgment with its recorded scope. It does not name a fact about the consensus of any research community.

## Tracking dependence and preventing double counting

<!-- id: c02-p029 -->
How is double counting prevented? It begins with sources. Sources are deduplicated by stable identifier first, and then by normalized title, authors, year and content. Preprints, published versions, corrections and supplements are linked to one another, and versions are distinguished wherever a relevant assertion changes between them. The reason is plain: an argument quietly strengthened between editions is a different argument. The same article hosted in different places is one source with several routes of access. It is not several sources, any more than ten copies of one photograph are ten photographs.

<!-- id: c02-p030 -->
Cases are deduplicated by the underlying person, event, date, site and chronology. Books, interviews, sermons and copied stories about one episode share a duplicate group and count once. A widely retold case is one case, however large its literature. Distinct episodes involving one person are recorded as related; they are not assumed to be independent. Apparent corroboration gets the same scrutiny. A new witness is tagged with relationship, exposure to information and the timing of the statement. Several witnesses repeating one briefing are not independent corroboration. They are one account with several voices.

<!-- id: c02-p031 -->
Studies and samples are checked for overlap by recruitment sites, enrollment dates, registry identifiers, participant counts and the authors' own statements about overlap. Secondary analyses, pooled studies and follow-ups are linked to the cohorts beneath them. Sample sizes are never added together where it has not been established that the samples are disjoint. Risks of dependence are tracked even where the participants differ, because shared recruitment channels and the cultural transmission of narrative motifs can entangle collections that are formally distinct. The same discipline extends to philosophy. Arguments that share a premise carrying real weight are assigned to a dependency group. The cumulative chapter therefore cannot count one premise's plausibility several times under several names.

<!-- id: c02-p032 -->
Rates and counts keep their anatomy. Every empirical rate keeps its numerator, its denominator, its unit of observation, its definition of eligibility, its timeframe, its exclusions and its missingness, with an exact locator. An unknown is never replaced with zero. A missing target opportunity is never scored as a failed test. Prospective studies are extracted along their full flow: from eligible events through enrollment, survival, eligibility for interview, consent, completed interviews, classified experiences, monitored cases and exposure to a target, to attempts at identification. Categories that a study does not report as nested are not forced into a single chain of attrition. Proportions are computed only from compatible units over justified denominators. Study-specific rates are kept before any aggregation, and pooling requires a separately justified contract.

<!-- id: c02-p033 -->
Double counting is controlled, finally, at the level of use. A single claim may bear on several hypothesis components, but it remains one observation however many components it touches. Within this thesis the control is built into the structure. Every substantive paragraph is mapped in a crosswalk to the specific claim, argument, case and source records, or the labeled premises, on which it rests. Reuse of one record across chapters is therefore visible and checkable instead of being diffused through the prose. The dependence map of the cumulative chapter draws on these crosswalks, and the whole-thesis review checks them for this very failure.

## Sensitivity testing and restraint about numbers

<!-- id: c02-p034 -->
Three assessments are kept separate for every claim, and they are never averaged. Record reliability asks whether this particular observation, report, quotation or attribution is documented with usable provenance, and it says whether the judgment applies to the existence of a report or to the event the report describes. Inference strength asks how well a particular conclusion follows from the record when serious alternatives are considered, with the bridge premises, the dependence and any decisive missing information stated. Component confidence asks what provisional assessment each of the nine hypothesis components warrants, given the reviewed evidence and objections. Each assessment uses the qualitative labels supported, contested, unresolved or challenged, with reasons and scope. The labels are not numerical bins. No arithmetic over them is defined, and none is used.

<!-- id: c02-p035 -->
These separations do real work. A reliable narrative may support only a weak metaphysical inference. That an account is well documented, early, stable and sincere settles nothing about whether its best explanation is exotic. An accurate attribution of a philosophical premise to its author is not evidence that the premise is true. And a claim's reliability label is never passed on as confidence in a hypothesis component. A record only supports a component through bridge premises that have to be stated, and those premises can themselves be contested. Nor is a task-level unresolved label converted into a cumulative prior, or into equal support for competing families.

<!-- id: c02-p036 -->
Numerical probabilities are permitted, but they are disciplined. They require explicitly specified hypotheses, defensible ranges for priors and likelihoods, stated assumptions about dependence, and sensitivity analysis. Overlapping metaphysical families are not forced to add up to one hundred percent, because the families are neither exclusive nor exhaustive in the way that would require. Where defensible likelihoods are unavailable, and in this evidence base they generally are unavailable, the thesis offers conditional qualitative comparisons and says plainly that the evidence does not identify a numerical posterior. No pooled posterior over cases or samples appears anywhere in this document. The denominators, the disjointness and the likelihoods that such a number would require were not established.

<!-- id: c02-p037 -->
Robustness is tested by withdrawal, not by weighting. The article's qualitative rule is simple: remove particular cases or premises and ask which reasons remain. The cumulative chapter carries that rule as eight prespecified sensitivity tests, executed at the accepted synthesis stage and re-read in the cumulative chapter against the fuller chapters. The first removes the strongest disputed named near-death case, selected by an explicit criterion rather than by convenience, with a second candidate shown wherever the ordering of strength is ambiguous. The second omits all retrospective cases and narratives, the retrospective community arm included, while keeping the truly prospective observations at their real denominators. The third removes contributions whose sample or event dependence is uncertain. The fourth varies the criteria of personal identity across psychological, organismic and simple-subject views, sets strict identity against prudential continuity, and varies the four recorded preservation models. The fifth removes the central bridge premises: endurance required for value, unity to obligation, and consciousness to authority. The sixth removes the global sufficient-reason premise, the epistemic-to-metaphysical premise and the life-valuing designer-preference premise, one at a time. The seventh removes the strongest selected neural-intervention constraint or report-to-experience proxy while keeping the independence model's actual predictive constraints, so that the countercase is stress-tested with the same severity as the case. The eighth restricts the comparison to directly inspected positive original defenders and their direct replies, and it excludes project reconstructions from any claim about authors. Each test reports its effect on each of the nine components separately.

<!-- id: c02-p038 -->
Stronger conclusions are gated, not hedged. The recorded synthesis constraints require every stronger intended conclusion to be mapped to the specific gap that blocks it. The project's register records thirty-five gap groups, each with an explicit condition for closing it, together with twelve withheld stronger-conclusion gates. All twelve are currently withheld. Before any chapter of this thesis asserts one of those conclusions, the blocking gap must have been repaired under review; otherwise the conclusion is explicitly withheld and its gate is cited. A generic caveat does not license a gated verdict. Appendix C carries the full register of dispositions, and the concluding chapters say which gates remain closed and what that costs the argument.

## What this method cannot deliver

<!-- id: c02-p039 -->
What can this method not deliver? Begin with coverage, which is bounded and asymmetric. Searches were budgeted, the screening windows were finite, the corpus is frozen at the publication cutoff, and the language scope skews cultural coverage toward material accessible in English. Several of these bounds are sharper than a reader of the protocol's requirements alone would infer. The destinations named in the search grid were planned venues, not claims of access to a database. Where a database was unavailable, the logs record a public web index queried in its place, and the two are not treated as the same thing. Engine rankings change over time and cannot be reproduced exactly; the logs preserve what was visible, not an experiment that can be rerun. And the obligation to run backward- and forward-citation passes was not met everywhere, beginning with the recorded opening exception. Nor can the historic searches be reconstructed. The lost early search work, the mixed pilot ranks and the uncaptured combined query described earlier are permanent losses, and no later search, however carefully logged, restores them. The citation network around the included sources is therefore bounded discovery, not a completed map. Nor is every position represented by an inspected original defender. The record distinguishes inspected originals, secondary attributions, project reconstructions and uninspected variants, and the comparative verdicts are restricted to match. In particular, no broad ranking of worldview families is asserted where a rival's strongest formulation was never directly inspected. Conclusions of that shape are either narrowed to the formulations actually examined or withheld under the register described above.

<!-- id: c02-p041 -->
The layers of enforcement and review have honest limits of their own. The closed taxonomies this chapter describes, six epistemic types and four assessment labels, bind as rules and as review criteria. But the ledger fields that carry them are free text, and they have drifted into a much larger vocabulary. Discipline lives in the judgment of reviewers, not in a schema that rejects a mislabeled record. The independent review this chapter describes is review by a distinct AI model context, under the same overseer, protocol and toolchain as the author. It is not re-inspection by human specialists. It includes no access to medical charts or private records, which no route in this project could authorize. The contracted independent final review of the article that this thesis expands remains unperformed. Its obligations pass, without weakening, into this thesis's chapter reviews and whole-thesis review, and until that closure the article's own conclusions keep the corresponding provisional status. The eight sensitivity tests were specified in full at the synthesis audit, which came after most of the evidence gathering. They are prespecified relative to the cumulative assessment they constrain, not relative to the collection of evidence. Finally, the public snapshot of this project excludes downloaded source files and raw audit materials. A public reader can verify the structure of the record and the reasoning. That reader cannot replay the inspections themselves.

<!-- id: c02-p040 -->
Let me be clear, finally, about what this method has not shown and cannot show. It cannot turn its own rigor into truth. Procedural acceptance, however disciplined, is a fact about this project's workflow. The requirements that a distinct reviewer reopen decisive sources and that disagreements be recorded raise the cost of error; they do not abolish it. The assessments that follow are qualitative, comparative and conditional. They identify no numerical posterior, certify no completed systematic review, and claim no external peer validation. What the discipline of this chapter establishes is a requirement, not an achievement. Every substantive conclusion in the following chapters must be traceable through the crosswalk to identified records and labeled premises, testable by the stated withdrawals, and checkable against the stated gates. A reader who rejects a conclusion can then locate which record, premise or bridge they are rejecting. Whether each chapter meets that requirement is decided by its own independent review, not by this chapter's description of the rule. And all twelve withheld stronger-conclusion gates remain withheld through this chapter, which opens none of them and asserts no conclusion they reserve.
