## Question and claim boundaries
State each perception, prediction, temporal experience or recovery claim separately. Identify which hypothesis components it could support if verified.

## Source provenance
Earliest accessible account; source IDs and precise locations; first report date; event date; retrospective versus contemporaneous record; access limits; shared reporting chains.

## Timeline and medical limits

## Corroboration and alternatives

For recovery: diagnosis, prognosis, treatment, durability and causal attribution.
