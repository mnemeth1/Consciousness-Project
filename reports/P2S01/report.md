The strongest common-foundation case begins with the experiential explanandum, seeks a justified explanatory ground, and asks whether one constrained conscious basis could unify lawful appearances and distinct persons. Specific crisis reports and prospective recall provide a further conditional line of inquiry: independently accurate acquisition during established exclusion conditions would challenge a specified ordinary-process model. Present documents do not establish that conjunction. Independent, specified agency expectations could add conditional force from life and order; worthwhile same-person continuation could connect intended development with lasting goods, and a justified good-nature/authority relation could connect value with obligations. These are possible mutually supporting explanatory relations. They are not established by source agreement, and assumptions of grounding, survival, goodness or authority cannot count as evidence for themselves. The programme gains developed attribution in Phase 2, not an independently confirmed nine-component package. [SS-P2S01-CUMULATIVE-POSITIVE; work/P2S01/decisive_source_crosswalk.json#/statements/92].

The strongest countercase combines developed physical/representational, emergent, plural and normative alternatives with specific constraints: phenomenal/constitution premises are contested; neural interventions constrain denial of neural contribution; crisis reports lack decisive content/timing/access conjunctions; unknown and reused samples preclude additive convergence. Human identity, eternal representation, intention and authority require further bridges. Suffering and nonresistant nonbelief challenge specified perfect-governance expectations, while finite fitting engagement challenges strict endurance necessity. This neither demonstrates an ordinary cause for every report nor proves every alternative equally credible. It shows why improved source coverage has not established the integrated package or eliminated the strongest selected rivals.
