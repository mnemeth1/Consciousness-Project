## Inference and countercase audit

**A-T11-001:** The physical-to-metaphysical boundary holds. A mathematical description containing an entire ordered history need not make all events simultaneous. Eternalism would still need an argument linking its ontology to a particular experiencing bearer. The packet also avoids the converse mistake of treating local change as a direct refutation of an atemporal ground.

**A-T11-002:** The strongest positive reconstruction posits an actual experiential ground, an explanatory relation to concrete history, reasons-guided intention and distinct dependent perspectives. This is more informative than a bare appeal to unexplained physics. Its conclusion remains conditional on these substantive assumptions. In particular, saying that a ground explains a history is not itself an explanation of why the history concretely exists or why its contents are experiential. Those burdens remain explicit.

**A-T11-003:** The objection concerns irreducible presentness and cognitive access, not mere dated facts. Its crucial third premise requires argument: a changing truth must require intrinsic cognitive succession in the knower. Rejecting objective tense blocks this particular objection; it does not by itself establish an atemporal mind. A non-successive access relation is an alternative proposal whose adequacy remains open. Knowing someone else's indexical belief need not make the knower that individual, but full knowledge of their perspective and objective presentness are separate questions.

**A-T11-004:** Having reasons need not be defined as having deliberated through successive states. Equally, a fixed relation called an intention need not automatically count as agency. The report retains this disagreement. Its response-to-request example expressly treats counterfactual differences between possible histories as different from new state acquisition in one actual foundation. That is a candidate account of responsiveness, not an established equivalent of noticing and revising. The temporal foundation alternative sacrifices H-T for that bearer without automatically sacrificing fundamentality or endurance.

**A-T11-005:** M1, M2 and M3 match the T01 stipulations. A complete finite history, represented memory, or another mind's awareness of a person alone does not satisfy the project's H-S condition. A richer first-person history must be judged under an explicit identity and beyond-death account. The packet leaves that work to T13 rather than dismissing such a model by terminology.
