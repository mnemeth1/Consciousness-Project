# P2T02 — Substance dualism: selected causal exchange

Submitted for independent review under protocol_v2. This packet repairs one specified source-coverage gap, subject to review. It does not identify a winning mind theory or establish a surviving subject. All 35 frozen input hashes matched before use. The exact source selection preceded substantive inspection; an annex added Bennett before her chapter was inspected.

The central result is a sharper conditional comparison. A theory can specify a persisting bearer without establishing that such a bearer exists. It can avoid a particular exclusion argument without explaining every causal relation it posits. These are different achievements, and neither supplies an observation after death.

## Selected sources and actual exchange

CL-P2T02-004, 007 and 008 preserve both the objection and the reply’s costs.

Her [author-hosted scan](https://www.karenbennett.org/_files/ugd/4d77f7_cfd57f52556749a09b2426217af88725.pdf) was downloaded and all 14 image-only pages visually inspected. The exchange is asymmetric: Lowe is directly criticized by Engelhardt, and Bennett is directly answered by Engelhardt. Engelhardt revises the account, including its independent-powers ambition (p. 1155 n. 12). This is not a later Lowe rebuttal. Bennett’s chapter also does not prove that every dualist must accept physical completeness (§1, p. 283), and permits a qualified necessitation option (§6.2, pp. 296–299). Those limits prevent a blanket verdict against dualism.

## Argument assessment

The strongest causal countercase is not a bare appeal to energy conservation. Given a sufficient physical process, the opponent asks why its occurrence does not also suffice for the occurrence fact that the mental explanation targets. The defender owes a reason that the extra explanatory role is both needed and causally effective. Merely using two descriptions cannot answer that question. A-P2T02-001 and A-P2T02-002 identify the premises and bridges; they do not certify physical completeness as an independently measured fact.

The strongest available reply challenges the inference from distinct bearers to independent sufficient contributions. If a defensible embodiment relation makes the relevant contributions dependent, the two-cause case may differ from two independently effective firing squads. The decisive burden then moves to that relation: it must explain why the dependence holds, determine which counterfactual changes are legitimate, and avoid defining the desired result into the background. A-P2T02-003 states that reply and its residual commitments. Avoiding this objection would still leave the distinct-substance ontology to justify.

My assessment is that these are substantive competing models of explanation, not independently confirmed causal mechanisms. The original-defender coverage is materially better than v1’s short bearer illustration. The inference to an actual extra substance remains underdetermined by the inspected arguments. In particular, a physical organization can persist while its parts change; the defender needs more than changing constituents to exclude that rival. Likewise, a unified experienced decision does not alone settle the metaphysical individuation of its cause. These are project objections, not claims that an uninspected author has conceded defeat.

Coherence is conditional intelligibility here, not a formal consistency theorem. Explanatory reach includes subjecthood, persistence and intentional action under stated assumptions; it does not cover every boundary between subjects, the onset of experience or cosmic intentions. Assumption costs include bearer identity, embodiment, event individuation and relevant counterfactual background. The physicalist must also justify its identity or realization relation under the same evidential standard. No empirical advantage is awarded simply because either side labels its preferred relation fundamental or physical.

Empirical compatibility is also limited. The inspected positions discuss neural dependence, but no new neural study or causal intervention was inspected. Lowe’s use of indeterminism must not be promoted into a verified neuroscience result or into proof of mental control. The packet supplies no tested discriminating prediction between these exact variants. Separate record reliability, inference strength and hypothesis confidence are therefore mandatory: direct attribution is supported, the model comparison is conditional, and no H-component confidence upgrade follows.

## Identity and conclusion boundaries

The fixed bearer is an individual continuing self. Identity means strict numerical identity, not resemblance, copied memory or the continuation of a pattern. The selected theory gives no independent postmortem identity test, no irreversible-death study endpoint and no observational rule choosing one survivor after branching. Ordinary interruption and resumption of awareness do not settle those questions. Animal, psychological and primitive-self identity assumptions must remain explicit before any later survival scoring.

All nine components remain separate. This exchange does not establish fundamental consciousness (H-F), a universal foundation (H-U), actual brain independence (H-B), survival (H-S), timelessness (H-T), preservation (H-P), cosmic intention (H-I), goodness (H-G), or authority (H-A). Preservation must distinguish information, accessible memory, relationships, subjectivity and value. An enduring bearer in a theory is not evidence of non-successive existence. None of the twelve frozen conclusion gates is closed.

Irreducibility (IR) is a model commitment here, not a proven route to fundamentality. For the cross-cutting existence question X-EX, the target remains individual subjecthood and bodily action: the inspected account does not derive why anything exists, why physical reality exists or why its laws obtain. A common conscious foundation remains a separate possible proposal requiring its own dependence account. No normative bridge is supplied by this packet.
