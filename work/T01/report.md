# T01 — Hypothesis specification and working glossary

Status: submitted for independent G0 review; no findings accepted. Version: T01 submission 1. Protocol: `bootstrap_framework_v1`. Date: 11 September 2026.

## 1. Purpose and epistemic status

This memo supplies project stipulations for comparing hypotheses. It does not establish that any proposed entity exists, that any metaphysical theory is coherent in all its details, or that any experience has been independently verified. The definitions and conditional argument skeletons are authored here, not attributed to external literature. No external searches or source inspections were used. Internal governing files were read and their contracted hashes verified.

The central proposal is: **conscious reality is basic, the physical universe and individual experience depend on it, and embodied lives may have enduring significance through a conscious foundation that is not confined to physical succession.** This is a family of hypotheses requiring explicit variants. Its full, strongest version adds universal subjecthood, personal survival, preservation, intention, goodness and moral authority. Supporting one component does not automatically support the whole conjunction.

The motivating question remains whether such a foundation explains existence, experience, enduring significance, cosmic purpose and morality better than specified alternatives. This memo makes that question testable in parts; it does not replace cosmic purpose with finite-life satisfaction.

## 2. Nine commitments and the plan/framework reconciliation

Use the following stable labels in task reports. Each commitment must specify its bearer: the foundation, every person, some persons, or another explicitly bounded population. An existential finding cannot silently become a universal claim.

| ID | Commitment and working test | Important limits and non-entailments |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H-F | **Fundamentality:** experiential properties or conscious reality belong to the basic ontology and are not wholly grounded in nonexperiential reality. Specify fundamental properties, individual subjects, or one foundation. | Irreducibility alone does not establish this stronger ontological status. Fundamentality does not entail metaphysical necessity, universality, cosmic explanation, survival, or intentional creation. |
| H-U | **Universality:** every individual subject in the claimed domain depends on one numerically single conscious subject or conscious whole. State whether the whole itself is a subject; these are distinct variants. | Shared laws, similar experiences, or common physical ancestry are not this claim. One whole does not entail timelessness, personal identity with the whole, intention, goodness, or moral authority. |
| H-B | **Brain independence:** a subject can instantiate experience without the bodily/neural processes that are necessary under the specified dependence model. State actual occurrence versus metaphysical possibility and temporary independence versus independence after irreversible bodily death. | Not synonymous with independence of all physical substrates. It does not establish that a particular person survives death, that a mind is fundamental, or that experience is timeless. Lack of a measurement is not satisfaction of this criterion. |
| H-S | **Personal survival:** the numerically same person has subjectivity beyond that person's irreversible bodily death, under an explicitly chosen identity relation. | An account of resuscitation alone does not satisfy the death condition. Resemblance, a stored record, or replacement by an indistinguishable subject is insufficient under strict numerical identity. Survival need not be timeless, universal, morally good, or unlimited in duration. |
| H-T | **Timelessness:** the specified bearer has no intrinsic temporal succession. Temporal effects or contents require a separately stated relation to that bearer. | Not mere longevity, eternity through successive times, reported loss of time awareness, or existence in a different time dimension. A timeless foundation does not entail timeless persons or survival. |
| H-P | **Preservation:** a specified item from embodied life remains available or has continuing significance. Record separately information, accessible memory, relationships, subjectivity and value. | Preservation of information need not preserve a subject. Surviving subjects need not remember all experiences. Preserved significance does not itself entail a storing mind, intrinsic goodness, or intended purpose. |
| H-I | **Intention:** a conscious agent directs or grounds the universe toward specified ends. Cosmic purpose here means an intended end for existence as a whole or a specified cosmic domain. | Producing consciousness does not by itself establish existing in order to produce it. Intention need not entail benevolence, success, necessity, or moral authority. An impersonal teleology is a separate alternative. |
| H-G | **Goodness:** the proposed foundation's nature or agency meets a stated normative standard of goodness, rather than merely having power or being desired by observers. | Goodness needs an explicit account; it cannot be defined as whatever the foundation happens to do. A good being need not create the universe or issue binding commands. Preserving experience, including suffering, need not be good on balance. |
| H-A | **Moral authority:** the foundation grounds genuinely binding moral reasons or obligations, with a specified normative relation connecting it to agents. | Power, authorship, unity, rewards, punishment, or command alone do not establish this relation. Authority is not automatically goodness, and objective moral value is not automatically a divine command or obligation. |

**Logical qualification:** the nine components are separately assessed, not asserted to be nine logically independent propositions. Under a version of H-S in which a person survives without any replacement brain, H-S entails that instance of H-B. Survival by a replacement physical substrate may be compatible with dependence on a brain of some kind. H-S entails continuing subjectivity under its identity rule, a narrow form of H-P; it does not entail complete informational, relational, or evaluative preservation. These consequences must be recorded when that version is selected.

The substantive plan has eight rows; the framework has nine components. The mapping below preserves both rather than dropping an explanatory question or silently adding a tenth required attribute.

| Research-plan row | Operational treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Fundamental consciousness | H-F; track irreducibility as a weaker subsidiary claim, IR, because an explanatory gap is not yet fundamentality. |
| Universal consciousness | H-U, with conscious-subject versus conscious-whole variant explicit. |
| Timelessness | H-T. |
| Endurance | H-B and H-S, because independence of current bodily function and survival of the same person are different claims. |
| Preservation | H-P, disaggregated by what is preserved. |
| Explanatory foundation | X-EX: cross-cutting explanatory target. Identify whether the claim concerns why anything exists, why physical reality exists, why its laws/structure obtain, or why consciousness occurs. Assess causal or grounding relations and explanatory stopping points explicitly. X-EX remains an indispensable question for the central package, but is not a newly numbered attribute. |
| Intention and purpose | H-I; keep intended end, achieved end and beneficial outcome distinct. |
| Moral foundation | H-G and H-A, while separately recording objective value and reasons. Goodness and authority do not exhaust every possible moral foundation account. |

The phrase “fundamental consciousness” will therefore never be used as shorthand for an adequate explanation of existence. Likewise, support for IR must not be entered directly as support for H-F without a bridge.

## 3. Necessary and optional packages

“Required” means required to count as the named hypothesis, not established truth. “Open” means unconstrained by that package, not denied. Versions are nonexclusive; no probability allocation should force them to sum to 100%.

| Package | Required commitments | Open commitments and additional specification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F-min: fundamental-experience thesis | H-F | H-U/B/S/T/P/I/G/A remain open. Does not itself explain physical existence. |
| CF: conscious-foundation thesis | H-F plus the claim that physical reality depends on conscious reality, with an X-EX grounding account | H-U/B/S/T/P/I/G/A open. Scope of explanation and remaining brute facts must be named. |
| TC: timeless conscious foundation | CF + H-T for the foundation | H-U/B/S/P/I/G/A open. Must explain temporal manifestation; does not promise personal survival. |
| ES: enduring personal subjectivity | H-S under an explicit identity model; corresponding narrow H-P and any model-dependent H-B consequences | H-F/U/T/I/G/A and richer preservation open. Temporal survival is allowed. |
| UP: universal preservation proposal | CF + H-U + H-P, specifying retained items | H-S is open unless subjectivity is explicitly included; H-T/B/I/G/A remain open. |
| CP: consciousness-purpose proposal | CF + H-I with experience among the independently specified ends | H-U/B/S/T/P/G/A open. Must distinguish intended production from incidental production. |
| Full integrated proposal | CF + H-U/B/S/T/P/I/G/A, with explicit bearers and compatible models | Necessity, omnipotence, omniscience, particular religious identity and unlimited duration for every person are further optional claims. No package includes them by default. |

For the full version, stipulate one timeless conscious foundation, personally surviving individuals related to it, preservation sufficient for the intended account of enduring significance, and a good purpose plus an account of moral authority. A timed individual need not share the foundation's timelessness. A subject can depend on a foundation without being numerically identical to it. Whether these relations can be coherently specified remains a research question.

Record a package revision when any required claim is withdrawn or a new mechanism is added. Supporting F-min after abandoning survival is a possible substantive result, not confirmation of the unchanged full proposal.

## 4. Operational time and identity models

Operational here means precise enough to classify claims, expose contradictions and ask discriminating questions. It does not promise a currently available measurement of metaphysical timelessness or numerical identity.

| Model | Succession, person and preservation | What must be supplied; evidential boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| M1: temporal personal continuation | The same subject continues in a sequence after bodily death. It may change, learn and remember. H-S can hold without H-T. | Specify substrate/ground, identity criterion and limits of duration. Memory continuity is potentially informative but not a sufficient proof of numerical identity. |
| M2: timeless foundation, temporal persons | The foundation lacks intrinsic succession; dependent individuals have ordered experiences, potentially beyond bodily death. The foundation includes or grounds their histories without itself acquiring new states. | Specify the grounding relation and how individuated perspectives arise. “Accumulation” refers to the individual's succession or inclusion of a history, not change in the foundation. H-S remains an additional claim. |
| M3: atemporal personal inclusion | A complete first-person life or enlarged subject-history is included without intrinsic succession at the foundational level. | Must distinguish ordered contents from successive awareness, and specify what “beyond death” means if no later subjective episode occurs. Mere inclusion of the finite life is H-P, not H-S. Count H-S only if the identity theory specifies subjectivity beyond the bodily-life boundary. |
| M4: preservation without personal continuation | Information, relationships as represented, or value is retained in a universal mind or another medium; the original person's subjectivity may cease. | State accessibility and fidelity of the retained item. Accurate reconstruction alone does not settle whether the original person exists. This model can satisfy parts of H-P while denying H-S. |
| M5: reconstruction or branching | A later system reproduces memories or psychological organization; multiple equally continuous successors are possible. | Separate strict identity from psychological-successor survival. Under strict numerical identity, two mutually distinct successors cannot both simply be the one numerically identical predecessor; a different identity relation or selection rule is required. Do not silently switch criteria. |
| M6: participation in a universal subject | Individual perspectives are expressions of a whole; their individuality may continue, transform, or dissolve. | Persistence of the whole is H-U, not automatically H-S for each person. Specify whether the very same individual subject persists or only universal subjectivity continues. |

The minimally required **identity declaration** for a later report is: bearer; bodily death criterion and whether it is actually established; strict numerical identity versus successor relation; what makes the relation hold; memory requirements; whether interrupted subjectivity is allowed; branching response; relation between personal and universal subjects; and whether the proposed continuation is temporal or atemporal. The project does not settle the correct identity theory by stipulation. Alternative declarations must be carried into the sensitivity analysis.

For temporal reports, code separately: perceived slowing/speeding; indeterminate duration; apparent absence of succession; simultaneity; and narrative order on later recollection. These describe a report's content. Inferring H-T requires a further bridge from phenomenal presentation to reality's temporal structure and assessment of alternatives. Evidence of future information, even if eventually established, would also require an argument identifying timelessness over temporal alternatives.

## 5. Working glossary

| Term | Working meaning and guardrail |
| --- | --- |
| Consciousness / phenomenal subjectivity | There being something it is like for a subject. An operational target, not a theory of its ultimate constitution. |
| Intelligence | Capacities for learning, inference, planning and problem solving. Such capacities and subjective experience must not be equated by definition. |
| Qualia | Features of experience as experienced. Using the term does not precommit the project to a particular ontology of those features. |
| Subject / person | A subject has a perspective; a person is the identity-bearing individual at issue. Personhood requirements beyond subjectivity remain model-specific. |
| Irreducible | Not reducible in the specified explanatory, conceptual or ontological sense. Always say which sense; one does not automatically establish the others. |
| Fundamental / necessary | Fundamental concerns ontological dependence; necessary means could not have failed to exist under the specified modality. Basic reality may be proposed as contingent. |
| Grounding / causal explanation | Grounding concerns what a fact or entity depends on metaphysically; causal explanation concerns production or difference-making. Do not assume grounding is an earlier event or rename an unexplained dependency an explanation. |
| Weak / strong emergence | For comparison, weak emergence is compatible with the base and its laws fixing the emergent facts without new fundamental psychophysical commitments; strong emergence adds an irreducible property or principle. T03 must give explicit versions and not rely on the label to solve subjectivity. |
| Mind–brain dependence | Specify correlation, intervention-sensitive dependence, necessary realization, or metaphysical identity. They are different claims. |
| Timeless / eternal | Timeless excludes intrinsic succession; eternal may mean unending temporal existence or timeless existence and therefore must be disambiguated. |
| Endurance / survival | Endurance means persistence under stated conditions. Personal survival means H-S under a declared identity theory. Neither word alone means forever. |
| Preservation | Retention of a named item, with fidelity, bearer, accessibility and duration explicit. Preserved information is distinct from preserved subjectivity. |
| Numerical / qualitative identity | Being the same individual versus having the same or similar properties. An exact qualitative duplicate does not establish numerical identity by description alone. |
| Subjective meaning | Significance experienced or endorsed within a life. Its finitude does not settle its value or cosmic purpose. |
| Objective value | Value that is not made true solely by a particular person's preferences. Its ontology and source remain open. |
| Enduring significance | Value or significance not exhausted by the finite occurrence of an experience. Specify whether this requires memory, continued subjects, continuing effects, or timeless value. |
| Cosmic purpose | An intended end for the universe or specified cosmic domain under H-I; do not infer it from an outcome's existence. Keep nonintentional teleology as a distinct alternative. |
| Moral reasons / obligations | Considerations genuinely counting in favor of action / binding requirements. Psychological motivation and causal origins of behavior are separate questions. |
| Creator / God | “Creator” identifies a proposed explanatory or originating role with its mechanism unstated. “God” is too underspecified for assessment until attributes and relations are declared. |
| Evidence / interpretation | Material bearing on a proposition / an account of what that material means. A verified report can remain evidence of reporting without verification of its narrated event. |

These stipulations are revisable at G0 or by a versioned protocol change. A rival using a different definition is evaluated under an explicit translation, not dismissed through the project's vocabulary.

## 6. Disputed bridge register

Each bridge is a premise to investigate, not an accepted inference. The table supplies the strongest motivation and a decisive vulnerability for the connecting work.

| ID | Proposed bridge | Motivation; strongest objection or alternative; what would help decide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| B01 | An unresolved explanatory gap implies ontological irreducibility or H-F. | Motivation: experience may require basic explanatory resources. Objection: an epistemic or conceptual gap need not be an ontological gap. Need an argument ruling out the relevant identity or realization account, not dissatisfaction alone. |
| B02 | H-F explains the physical universe (CF / X-EX). | Motivation: a common experiential basis may unify experience and reality. Objection: fundamental experience can coexist with unexplained physical facts. Need an explicit dependence account and its explanatory remainder. |
| B03 | H-F or CF implies H-U. | Motivation: one foundation may seem parsimonious. Objection: many basic subjects or non-subjective experiential properties remain possible. Need an individuation and combination/decomposition account; the word “one” does not establish simplicity. |
| B04 | H-B implies H-S. | Motivation: weakening dependence on present bodily function opens a possibility of survival. Objection: temporary independence or another subject's experience does not establish same-person persistence after death. Need timing, identity and death premises. |
| B05 | H-P or H-U implies H-S. | Motivation: a universal foundation might retain lives in first-person form. Objection: retained records and continued universal awareness may leave the individual extinct. Need the identity declaration and a subject-preserving relation. |
| B06 | Altered temporal phenomenology or H-S implies H-T. | Motivation: experience without familiar time might reflect a different ontology. Objection: altered experience of time and temporal survival are alternatives. Need a discriminating account of succession, not an ambiguous word “timeless.” |
| B07 | H-T permits knowing, intending and grounding temporal change. | Motivation: an entire ordered history could be included without updating. Objection: knowing-now, response, learning or deliberation may require change under some models. Need a coherent account distinguishing contents, relations and intrinsic states; no contradiction is assumed in advance. |
| B08 | Consciousness is produced, therefore H-I. | Motivation: conscious life may explain the point of a consciousness-producing universe. Objection: an outcome is compatible with an unintended byproduct. Need independently specified ends and consequences concerning distribution, suffering and preservation. |
| B09 | H-S/H-P is necessary or sufficient for objective or greater meaning. | Motivation: endurance could secure relationships and understanding against loss. Objection: finite value could be real, while endless suffering or repetition need not be better. Separate necessity, sufficiency and comparative-value premises; test each rather than treating longing as evidence. |
| B10 | H-U yields moral reasons or H-A. | Motivation: shared subjectivity may make others' welfare immediately relevant. Objection: unity by itself is descriptive; self-interest differs from obligation. Need a normative bridge from interests or suffering to reasons. |
| B11 | Creator status, power or command yields H-A. | Motivation: dependence may be proposed to establish obligations. Objection: origin and power alone leave moral justification open. Need an account explaining binding authority without mere threat or stipulated obedience. |
| B12 | Necessary goodness grounds H-A without arbitrariness or circularity. | Motivation: a good nature may anchor reasons rather than arbitrary commands. Objection: identifying goodness through that nature may be circular or leave the normative connection unexplained. Need an independent characterization or a defensible account of explanatory primacy. |
| B13 | A proposed purposeful foundation is good (H-I to H-G). | Motivation: some ends might explain flourishing or development. Objection: intention also permits indifferent or harmful ends; suffering and hiddenness challenge some good-purpose versions. Need the specified goodness, agency and purpose model, not generic creator claims. |
| B14 | Shared success across domains yields strong support for the full conjunction. | Motivation: a single independently motivated explanation could unify domains. Objection: extra attributes and reused premises can manufacture apparent convergence. Need the later dependence map and tests removing disputed evidence, identity criteria and moral/meaning bridges. |

No finite opening register can anticipate every future argument. A downstream task must add a bridge ID if its inference needs a premise not listed here; it cannot label a new bridge a definitional consequence to bypass review.

## 7. Handoff and acceptance boundaries

T03 can use this submission to build fair, explicit alternatives. G0 should review whether the nine-component mapping retains scope, whether CF's explanatory relation is sufficiently specified for the next stage, and whether identity/time distinctions prevent moving the target. The proposal deliberately leaves truth, coherence of fully developed models, comparative evidence and confidence unassessed.

The accompanying proposed ledger contains conditional argument skeletons, not evidence records or asserted metaphysical findings. There are no empirical claims, source records, case records, search records, or completed reviews to merge. The skeletons identify central disputed transitions for downstream argument work. G0 may accept these as method material while leaving every premise unresolved.

Specific unresolved choices: universal subject versus experiential whole; strict personal identity versus successor continuity; the bearer and scope of timelessness; what adequate preservation of significance requires; and a noncircular account of moral goodness and authority. Keeping these as explicit variants is deliberate, not a hidden completion claim. No decisive evidence was inaccessible because no external evidence was needed for this stipulated specification.

Recommended next action: submit T01 and T02 to T03 as drafts, then independent G0 review of T01–T03. Freeze reviewed definitions with the evidence protocol only through the overseer. Do not launch empirical truth claims on the authority of this memo.
