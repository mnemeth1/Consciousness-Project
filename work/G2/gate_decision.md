## Why conditional synthesis is eligible

Every scheduled domain has an independently reviewed, explicitly bounded packet. Prospective, retrospective, historical/cultural, distressing/conflicting and comparison-state evidence all now have inspected representation. Their ascertainment, denominators and documentation differ substantially. A bounded synthesis can expose those differences and ask what follows under explicit assumptions without certifying the reported events or generalizing to a population.

The selected philosophical packets contain substantive positive accounts and serious challenges. They can support a useful comparison of actual inspected arguments, and project reconstructions can clarify conditional possibilities when labeled. Accurate attribution and disciplined inference do not establish premise truth. A single common foundation may offer unification; the extra grounding, individuation, survival, intention and normative premises must remain visible rather than counted as new observations.

The framework expressly allows a scoped provisional gate when access gaps prevent full coverage. That provision is not a license to erase feasible missing literature: P1 repairs below are prerequisites for the particular stronger conclusions they block. T16 should identify those dependencies; T17/T18 must either complete the relevant repair under independent review or explicitly withhold the broader conclusion. Generic caveats appended to a full-ranking conclusion would not satisfy this gate.
