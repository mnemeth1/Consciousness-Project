# Consciousness and Existence: Provisional Synthesis

**T18 submitted draft — independent review of T16–T18 and final G3 adjudication remain pending.** This synthesis uses the reviewed, scoped evidence corpus and explicitly unreviewed dependency and sensitivity analyses. It adds no new source inspection or empirical finding. Methodological acceptance of a record establishes its documented scope, not the truth of its metaphysical interpretation.

The proposed conscious foundation remains a substantive conditional explanation worth investigating. It could connect subjective experience, lawful physical reality and enduring personal significance if its grounding and continuity relations can be independently defended. The present corpus does not establish the full package, or a broad winner among the strongest alternatives. Its clearest empirical positive result is local causal neural contribution under specified reports and behavioral proxies. Its clearest conceptual result is that fundamentality, universal dependence, survival, timelessness, preservation, purpose, goodness and authority require different warrants.

That assessment leaves real questions open. Failure to establish an ordinary cause of a particular near-death report does not establish independence from neural processes. Conversely, showing that brain interventions affect reports or arousal does not establish metaphysical identity or a complete explanation of experience. Unresolved actuality is not equal probability, and conditional explanatory promise is not empirical confirmation.

## The best cumulative case

Subjective experience presents an explanatory target beyond simply listing discriminations, reports and functions. In the selected Chalmers arguments, the connection between physical organization and experience requires further explanation, with modal and psychophysical premises supporting an irreducible account. A conscious-foundation proposal takes this target seriously by putting experience into the basis of reality. This has positive philosophical motivation; it is not merely a reaction to unusual experiences. Whether an epistemic gap licenses an ontological conclusion, and whether irreducibility requires fundamentality rather than strong emergence, remain contested steps.

A common conscious basis could then explain why private experience and shared physical regularity coexist. The best version states how distinct subjects depend on the whole and how their embodied perspectives track physical interventions. One set of constrained relations doing work across these questions would be an explanatory gain. Merely naming every unexplained relation a manifestation of consciousness would not produce that gain. Universality here includes dependence on one conscious subject **or conscious whole**; finite persons need not be numerically identical to it. A conscious creator can satisfy the relevant dependence claim in its specified created-subject domain.

The existential argument can add a different question: why any concrete order exists. Under a defended principle of sufficient reason and appropriate grounding premises, a necessary foundation could answer a demand left unmet by describing mechanisms. A mental foundation requires further premises. Likewise, specified life-valuing intentions may give order or conscious life conditional qualitative support for a creator relative to a specified rival. This comparison depends on substantive preferences and observation-selection assumptions; absence of a numerical likelihood does not make every qualitative comparison worthless.

Finally, actual same-person continuation in worthwhile relationships could preserve goods that extinction would end. A purposive and good foundation could connect those goods with a larger reason for embodied life. A defensible normative relation might then connect that foundation to obligations. This is the strongest integrated attraction of the proposal: a possible explanation of several connected questions. Survival, good purpose and legitimate authority are still additional commitments. Assuming restoration to defend goodness, then counting the defense as evidence for survival, would count the same assumption twice.

## The best cumulative countercase

The selected neural evidence is positive evidence, not simply a list of objections to extraordinary claims. Human fusiform stimulation altered reported face appearance under the study's controls. Central-thalamic stimulation under maintained anesthesia altered behavioral arousal and associated signatures in the two intervention macaques. Those different targets support local causal contributions; neither experiment measures every condition necessary and sufficient for consciousness. Report and awareness proxies remain explicit, and shared or repeated observations do not increase the number of independent subjects.

The strongest selected physical-identity response also addresses the explanatory gap directly: distinct phenomenal and physical concepts may concern the same property. That position needs positive identity warrant, but it cannot fairly be represented as simply ignoring experience. Basic psychophysical laws provide another live possibility: irreducible experience may lawfully depend on embodied organization without detachable persons. These alternatives constrain any inference from the inadequacy of one explanation to a universal conscious foundation.

The empirical anomalies do not currently close the relevant inferential gap. The reviewed record contains distinctive reports, technical disagreement and prospective attempts, but does not identify a complete case-specific chain from accurate acquisition, to independently fixed acquisition time, to exclusion of the processes required by a specified neural model and ordinary information routes. Missing evidence is a reason to withhold that inference; it is not a demonstrated ordinary explanation of each report.

The full package also accumulates burdens that a minimal experience thesis does not face. One ground must explain individuation; survival must cross an irreversible-death boundary with a defensible identity relation; timelessness must specify its bearer and relation to temporal contents. A perfectly powerful, knowledgeable and loving governor faces substantive suffering and hiddenness challenges. None of these challenges by itself refutes every fundamental-experience or minimally good-foundation variant. Together they explain why support for the richer conjunction cannot be inherited from a single appealing component.

## What the near-death evidence currently permits

Prospective recruitment improves ascertainment but does not turn later recollection into a contemporaneous experience measurement. AWARE I includes an author-reported corroborated event and an inferred interval from an AED sequence, without patient-specific neural evidence of absent processing. Its two event-recall locations lacked installed target shelves. AWARE II does not identify the confirmed target-exposed/interviewed intersection; its two interviewed participants with EEG data were not the explicit-recall participants. Aggregate EEG descriptions therefore cannot be attached to a remembered episode to prove its time or neural independence. Interview groups, targets, monitoring and selected community survivors must retain their different denominators and roles.

The Dutch prospective cohort and the dentures anecdote also have different evidential roles. Their documentary connection does not establish that the anecdote is an additional confirmed cohort participant. The dentures material remains a principal-witness reporting chain across interviews and replies. For Reynolds, the accepted timing assessment places the checkable early perceptions before circulatory standstill and absent brainstem response; later experiential contents are not thereby time-locked. Hearing and other ordinary explanations remain debated possibilities, not established causes. The earliest public account, clinical timing and direct-reply gaps remain material.

Neal's rescue and prediction accounts retain attribution and dating limitations; Alexander's severe illness and published clinical review do not fix when the reported experience occurred or establish a zero-processing interval. Moorjani remains a screened, deferred lead rather than an extra corroborated acquisition result. Selected historical, cultural and distressing reports broaden the kinds of reports represented, but do not establish global motif invariance, prevalence or independent pooled convergence.

## Existence, meaning and the purpose of embodied lives

A fundamental experience thesis does not yet explain existence. The proposal must say what is grounded, how the grounding works and why its own stopping point is acceptable. The same demand applies to physical primitives, basic psychophysical laws and experiential constituents. Counting one foundation as one entity while ignoring its independently adjustable laws, intentions and individuation relations would overstate simplicity. Requiring every conscious primitive to be explained while exempting physical primitives would be equally asymmetric.

Meaning also requires more than one question. Finite benefit can have finite significance under an explicit value premise without resolving the user's question about enduring cosmic significance. Wolf's account of active engagement with worthy objects expressly leaves room for that further concern. Craig's ultimate-significance proposal positively connects God and immortality but does not itself establish their actuality. It should be assessed as a stronger specified demand, not confused with the denial that any kindness, understanding or relationship has local value.

Worthwhile continuation could enhance significance by preserving persons and their projects. The necessity claim—that loss of all enduring bearers defeats every relevant kind of significance—needs a further principle. Williams's argument concerns categorical motivation and a future appropriately connected with present aims, not simply an unpleasant feeling of boredom. Fischer's response proposes varied activities, repeatable goods and tolerable character change. Thus neither mere identity nor mere endlessness guarantees a desirable life; rejecting inevitable tedium does not establish survival.

Purpose variants make different claims: production of consciousness, intentional production, development, same-person preservation, preservation of history, impersonal teleology, incidental consciousness and a local designer. These cannot be traded for one another when challenged. Actual development must be distinguished from deprivation rationalized after the fact. Preservation of a history need not preserve its subject. An intended end may explain a role while leaving its worth unjustified. Any route to cosmic purpose requires evidence or argument for agency, the specified end and its actual grounding relation, as well as normative premises where goodness is claimed.

M1 allows temporal continuation. M2 allows temporal persons grounded in a timeless foundation. Finite M3 whole-history inclusion does not add further experience; enlarged M3 can include actual beyond-death subjectivity only by adding that condition and an identity relation. M4 can preserve a record while its subject ceases. Psychological continuity, animal persistence and a simple bearer set different identity requirements; none supplies observed beyond-death subjectivity by stipulation. Valuable successor concern is a different target from strict numerical survival.

## Dependence and the tests that change the assessment

The T16 graph distinguishes conjunctive required premises within a route from alternative routes, evidential anchors, challenges and target-restating conditions. It does not mechanically infer component truth. A universal grounding rule, an actual store or an actual restoration condition can specify what would make a model work without being evidence that it does work. T17 tests these warrants from a common baseline, not a chain of accumulating removals.

The dependence map preserves shared philosophical lineages and case/reporting chains. Chalmers and Papineau used in two tasks are not two independent debates. AWARE access versions and shared datasets are not replications. Repeated witness accounts remain one chain. Uncertain IANDS/Liège or comparator overlap is not assumed to be either duplication or independence. The dentures link does not alter Dutch cohort counts. The same normative premise reused for meaning, purpose and goodness is still one premise.

Seventeen scenario rows implement eight sensitivity designs. Separate Reynolds removal, dentures removal and joint removal narrow the anomalous-detail case while also removing each case's own counterarguments. Removing all retrospective narratives and the selected community arm leaves prospective recruitment but not a contemporaneous measure of experience. Mixed summaries are qualified contribution by contribution so removed evidence cannot reappear through an aggregate. Within-person drug/placebo evidence remains distinct from a withdrawn retrospective comparator.

Identity and history variants change the needed evidence without creating observed survival. Rejecting the endurance-necessity premise removes that stronger meaning claim while leaving enhancement and finite value conditional claims. Rejecting unity-to-obligation removes its normative extension without implying nihilism. Those two premises are explicit T17 overlays, not required-input edges falsely deleted from T16. Rejecting AUTH, PSR, MODAL or EXPECT disables the respective authority, necessary-ground, modal-gap or design-expectation route. Dependent routes can lose indirect warrant while retaining alternative conditional structure; no component is automatically false.

Removing the human or macaque intervention separately weakens different neural warrants. Weakening human report-to-experience interpretation applies to NDE reports as well as stimulation reports; observed reporting remains. Animal arousal-to-awareness interpretation is tested separately. The original-only attribution test preserves exact inspected positions and labeled reasoning, while withdrawing any appearance that missing rivals were defeated in their strongest authored form.

The broad confidence labels mostly remain unresolved because the stronger actuality thresholds were not met at baseline. That stability is not robust confirmation of the package, equal evidential value of its inputs, or evidence that the tested premises do no work. The material change is which conditional reasons survive. The full executed operations and every route/component result remain in T17 for independent challenge.

## What would discriminate, and what remains withheld

The next useful empirical advance would connect an independently recorded report to prespecified target exposure, individual physiology and a narrowly defined acquisition interval, with ordinary information access assessed. Neither an inaccessible chart nor an unmeasured interval can be filled by inference. A successful literature repair would clarify a debate; it would not retroactively manufacture missing measurements.

For the metaphysical routes, progress requires constrained grounding and individuation rules, a defended modal or necessary-ground bridge, and meaningful comparisons with directly inspected rival originals. Survival needs actual further same-person subjectivity and a defensible identity criterion. Timelessness needs an account of a non-successive bearer and temporal contents rather than a duration report. Design needs independently motivated ends and comparative expectations. Moral authority needs a normative relation; goodness defenses need to distinguish actual restoration from coherence and permission from compensation.
