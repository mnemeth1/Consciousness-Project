The challenge and reply roles

Supplement §11 presents the consortium's interpretation; §12 is the IIT statement by Boly, Koch and Tononi; §13 is Dehaene's GNWT statement. These are contemporaneous responses to the study, not later rebuttals to Naccache's October 2025 commentary. They share the same experimental sample and include theory-proponent involvement; three discussions do not constitute three independent tests.

The consortium treats failed predictions as challenges to theories or their biological implementations, while noting that successes shared with other theories are not uniquely confirming. The IIT authors acknowledge the absent sustained-synchrony result and propose electrode coverage, frequency, and ROI limitations. Their claim of posterior sufficiency is an interpretation of correlational recordings, not a direct cortical inactivation result.
