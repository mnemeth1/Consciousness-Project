## Why

After thesis-v1.0.1 was published, a review of the released text against the ledgers, the gate records and the live pages (15 September 2026; three independent readers, every item verified against the record) found no sentence asserting a withheld conclusion and no mechanical defect, but nine should-fix items of record fidelity and a set of reader-visible defects in the generated References:

1. Two errors introduced in 1.0.1: the S-T10-002 bibliography note named a publisher ("Everyman") the record does not carry, and apB-p010 pointed the D5 citation at the appendix's opening paragraph instead of the paragraph that states the rule.
2. Pre-existing record-fidelity errors: c06-p022 and the Appendix A Reynolds row called the monitoring technologist "herself" where S-T19-007 records "he"; c06-p038 misdescribed the distressing-experience typology of CL-T08-014; c08-p022 sent suffering to "the next chapter" (chapter 9) when it is in chapter 10; c02-p041 placed the synthesis audit "after most of the evidence gathering" when the ledger puts it between the two phases.
3. Appendix C row G2-GAP-16 named c06-p037 as stating the tunnel-critique limitation; the paragraph did not. Because the register was adopted at P3G1, a fix needs a recorded amendment, not a silent edit, and the finding meant every row had to be re-checked.
4. S-T10-006 (Sober 2009) still read "abstract, notes and references only" although P2T11 had read the complete published scan under that identity and chapter 5 says so.
5. `work/P3M01/report.md` (public) still described the delta review as not yet run; `state/thesis_release.json`'s pipeline string still said "under tag thesis-v1.0.0".
6. References: eleven DOI locators printed unlinked, one raw `http://` locator, one empty author segment, doubled punctuation after titles ending in "?" or ".".
7. Minors checkable against a named record: c04-p027's stale future tense; c03-p038 "receive dependency groups" for three shared premises that no dependency group carries; "preservation models" for T01's time-and-identity models (c02-p037, c11-p016); c08-p025's "chapters on either side" (chapter 9 carries no existence question); c06-p035 "establishes" for a single medieval narrative; apC-p009 and c11-p034 "the two author replies … listed as its standing request" (T17's standing request was one reply).
8. Two crosswalk documentation gaps the distinct-model reviewer had recorded as a process defect in the 1.0.1 delta review: apC-p001 and apC-p018 named gate records their crosswalk entries did not cite.
