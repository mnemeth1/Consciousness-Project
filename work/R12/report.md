## Comparative reasoning

The strongest positive strategies are present: warranted identification, general psychophysical law, experiential constitution and common conscious grounding. Their burdens are assessed using the same C1–C6 questions. The packet neither demands that only conscious primitives explain their own existence nor rewards physical identity without requiring warrant. It treats epistemic difference and ontological difference separately.

Combination and decomposition are not reduced to an information-processing binding problem. The report preserves the distinction between phenomenal contents and their bearer, and the different consequences of identity with a whole versus grounding by it. The modal premise is treated symmetrically. A missing explanation is not presented as a demonstrated contradiction.

Closure/exclusion is a conditional causal package, not an energy-conservation slogan. The report/knowledge reply is identified as project reconstruction and retains its justification burden. Substance interactionism is distinguished from the directly inspected property-dualist defense. Idealism is given a shared-world proposal rather than caricatured as individual wish fulfillment; an appearance mapping still needs independent constraints.

All nine component assessments preserve assumptions, dependence, sensitivity and questions. Cosmic persistence is not personal survival; stability of a kind is not absence of intrinsic succession; experiential primacy does not supply normative authority. These are limited entailment checks, not a negative metaphysical verdict. Neural dependence is imported from accepted T09 with measurement/report limitations, not recounted as new evidence.
