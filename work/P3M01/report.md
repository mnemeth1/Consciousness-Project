## Why

After thesis-v1.0.0 was published, a review of the live pages (Claude on the web, 14 September 2026; verified against the repository in the project record) found that the release pass had closed the gates and regenerated the banner without a consistency sweep of the prose, the bibliography rendering or the site entry pages:

1. The live start page (`index.html`) still said P2R20 and P2G2 "remain pending" and that the cumulative chapter, conclusion and appendices were "not yet written"; the article and companion navigation still labeled the thesis "Incomplete thesis expansion".
2. Twelve citation brackets rendered as raw text (`[@A-T01-001]`… in c01-p010; multi-source `[@S-a; @S-b]` in c06-p014, c06-p015, c06-p039 ×2, c07-p020, c07-p023, c07-p036, c08-p006), because `scripts/thesis/build.cjs` matched only the single form. Seven cited sources were therefore absent from References (S-P2T13-001, S-P2T14-002, S-P2T14-008, S-P2T18-004, -005, -006, -007) and the build's citation count read 128 instead of 135.
3. Status statements true at drafting and false at release remained in c00-p002 (Declarations: "remains unperformed … has not been run … this is a working draft"), c02-p041, c03-p035 ("not drafted at this writing"), c11-p035 (Limitations: "have not been run", "remain unaccepted"), c12-p025 ("working draft until its own gates close"), apB-p010 ("until it is accepted"); c02-p014's dated 41-of-46 count was never followed by the closure; c01-p007/p008 repeated a clause across a style-pass seam.
4. `CURRENT_RELEASE.md`, `STATUS.md`, `README.md`, `records/README.md`, `thesis/README.md` and `state/public_status.json` contradicted the release records or themselves.

Findings the review raised that were not defects: `paper-stage=draft` on the article (by design; P3G2 §4); the 2026-09-15 stamp (UTC release date); Weisberg 2010 (disclosed as uninspected in c05 and Appendix C); Enoch 2014 §§ cites (journal PDF inspected). Answered by the record: T17 labels (R18 accepted T16–T18 on 2026-09-12); deferred minors (P3G2 §5 → `state/adjudication_P3RX.json`); the 46/46 count.

## What was done

**Renderer and lints (`scripts/thesis/build.cjs`).** Multi-source and record-reference citations render (single-source form byte-identical); any unrendered `[@` fails the build; every inline citation must appear in the paragraph's crosswalk entry; `bibliography_note` renders; in the released stage a narrow phrase lint rejects draft-time status language unless the paragraph is allowlisted with a reason in `thesis/thesis.json`. On the unmodified 1.0.0 text the lint flagged exactly c00-p002, c02-p041, c03-p035, c11-p035 and c12-p025 and nothing else. References: 128 → 135 entries.

**Prose (`changes.json`, applied by `apply_edits.py`, Lennox voice).** Fourteen clause edits in thirteen paragraphs: c00-p002, c01-p008, c02-p014, c02-p041, c03-p035, c11-p001, c11-p035, c12-p025, apB-p002, apB-p010, apC-p001, apC-p011, apC-p018. Thirteen crosswalk changes: premise labels re-pointed from STATUS.md / "submitted, not accepted" / the regenerable derived path to the gate records (`state/acceptance_P3G1.json`, `state/acceptance_P3R20.json`, `state/acceptance_P3G2.json`, `work/P3G2/gate_decision.md`), plus two premises added to apB-p010 for its frozen-v1 and C5-correction sentences. Mechanical invariants on every before/after pair: citations identical; no record id, number or date lost (c12-p025 drops the "frozen version 1" delta clause deliberately, see D17); no em-dash introduced; no new sentence over 45 words (apB-p010's one long sentence is pre-existing and untouched); all twelve chapter closings still state that all twelve gates remain withheld; `node scripts/thesis/build.cjs --check` passes with the released-stage lint active.

**Ledger housekeeping (`records/decisions.json` P3-LEDGER-001).** S-T10-002 gains the V-P2T04-004 version entry, locators and a bibliography note; S-T14-003 and S-T19-005 gain provenance notes; `state/T16_T18_status_note.json` records the submission-time labels as superseded; the derived layer regenerated (10 files changed, all traceable to the three annotated records).

**Site and status documents.** `paper/landing.html`, `paper/companion.html`, `paper/paper.html` (0.4.6; released-thesis label; corrected status text), `CURRENT_RELEASE.md`, `STATUS.md`, `README.md`, `records/README.md`, `thesis/README.md`, `state/public_status.json`; `scripts/validate_public_snapshot.cjs` now fails if an entry page calls the thesis incomplete or P2R20/P2G2 pending after closure; `docs/PAPER_RELEASE.md` gains a stage-transition checklist.

**Release records.** `thesis/thesis.json` 1.0.1 (crosswalks likewise); `paper/draft-release.json` 0.4.6 with the new page hashes; `paper/CHANGELOG.md` 0.4.6 entry; `state/acceptance_P3G2.json` amendment P3G2-A1 (review status pending); `state/thesis_release.json` 1.0.1 with the page hash and a placeholder P3G2 hash that `finalize.cjs` replaces after the review accepts.
