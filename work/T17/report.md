# T17: Conditional confidence and sensitivity assessment

Status: submitted analytical draft. Independent R18 review of T16–T18 is pending. This packet uses accepted frozen evidence records and the unreviewed T16 dependency-map draft. It performs no new source inspection, numerical posterior, route-count weighting or metaphysical acceptance.

## Outcome

The selected corpus gives comparatively firmer warrant for documented reports, measured local neural contributions and conceptual distinctions than for the full metaphysical package. Fundamental consciousness has substantive conditional philosophical motivations; one universal subject or conscious whole, personal survival, an actual timeless bearer, cosmic preservation, intention and authority require additional independent bridges. This is not a claim that all these propositions have equal probability or that no qualitative argument favors them.

Worthwhile continuation could add enduring significance. Its desirability is not evidence that continuation happens; rejecting endurance as necessary for every kind of meaning leaves enhancement intact.

## Identity and preservation

| Variant | Consequence for unchanged survival target |
| --- | --- |
| Psychological causal continuity, with nonbranching or independently defended unique-branch rule | Conditional only. Causal quasi-memory/continuity must not presuppose the identity being proved; actual further experience beyond bodily death remains needed. |
| Persistence of the same human animal, with explicit biological cessation boundary | Under a cessation/no-resumption criterion, an irreversibly ceased organism cannot persist as the same animal. If a resurrection/restoration model is proposed, independently specify why the animal itself persists or returns; this corpus supplies no such observed case. |
| Numerical identity of a simple subject or further-fact bearer | Conditionally permits continuity through memory loss and biological death, but must independently warrant the bearer, its determinate identity and actual experience beyond death. Stipulating a soul is not observing one. |
| Strict numerical identity is the unchanged project target | All candidate criteria must satisfy same-person subjectivity beyond irreversible bodily death; interrupted consciousness alone neither proves nor disproves identity. |
| Prudential psychological continuity or successor concern | Renamed target X-T17-SUCCESSOR-CONCERN; it does not satisfy unchanged H-S merely by being valuable or allowing branching. |

M1 allows temporal continuation; M2 allows temporal persons with a timeless foundation. Finite M3 inclusion does not add further subjectivity; enlarged M3 can satisfy survival only by adding actual further same-person experience. M4 preserves records/value without necessarily preserving the original subject. The apparent M3 success is a conditional specification, not evidence for its actuality. All variants retain the irreversible-death boundary. Same-person survival implies narrow subjectivity preservation; brainless survival implies brain independence only under the specified realization model.

## Contributions and graph qualifications

The same event is withdrawn across retellings and access versions. Mixed T19 summaries retain unaffected case content and independently warranted inferential boundaries. Removing dentures does not remove or alter the Dutch cohort; its anecdote is not another independently confirmed participant. Removing fusiform or macaque stimulation also qualifies the aggregate CL-T09-019 claim; retained causal intervention and associative evidence are kept distinct. DMT within-person drug/placebo evidence survives retrospective-comparator removal, but the comparator-based similarity inference does not.

BP-T16-NECESSITY and BP-T16-UNITYNORM have no T16 required-input edges. Their rejection is executed against explicit T17 argumentative extensions; it does not falsely sever an existing support route. BP-T16-AGENCY is separately identified as the actual-purpose grounding condition, while RT-T16-I-ORDER remains candidate-conditioned abduction. Rejection of AUTH, PSR, MODAL and EXPECT disables A-NATURE, F-GROUND, F-GAP and I-ORDER respectively. This is loss of that route, not proof of a component negation.

Human-report proxy skepticism applies to NDE reports as well as neural-intervention reports. Observed speech, behavior and neural effects remain; their phenomenal interpretation weakens. Animal awareness is tested separately. Weaker measurement does not establish extracerebral awareness.

The original-only test preserves labeled project reasoning and exact source attributions. It does not promote a reconstructed comparison cell to an original-author result. Cell-level anchors and excluded upgrades are listed in `sensitivity_results.json`; missing originals remain genuine coverage limits.

## Strongest cumulative cases

The best conditional common-foundation case takes phenomenal experience as a real explanatory target, proposes one constrained experiential ground for concrete lawfulness and distinct subjects, and adds same-person continuation, worthwhile goods, purposive agency and an independently defensible normative relation. If these conditions acquire independent warrant, the package could connect otherwise separate explanatory questions. Its potential gain is shared explanatory structure, not nine independent confirmations. NDE details supply questions about acquisition; they presently do not establish the exclusion/timing steps needed to carry the package.

Selected neural interventions give positive evidence of embodied causal contributions under their stated targets/proxies. Retrospective timing, information access and shared reporting limit extraordinary-acquisition inference, while prospective records leave key timing/opportunity intersections unresolved. Conceptual arguments do not force the further transitions to one subject, survival, non-succession, agency or authority. Perfect-governance versions face substantive suffering/hiddenness challenges. Developed physical-identity, psychophysical and naturalist-moral alternatives remain live within inspected scopes; missing stronger originals prevent a broad winner. This does not establish a complete neural explanation, an ordinary cause of every NDE, absence of finite value, or equality of hypotheses.

The positive case can gain explanatory integration if the added relations are independently warranted and mutually coherent. The countercase has specific empirical and inferential force; it does not become a total metaphysical refutation. Restoration cannot be assumed to defend goodness and then counted as new evidence of survival; inferred designer preferences cannot be used as an independent confirmation of the designer.
