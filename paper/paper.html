Phase 2 working draft: Consciousness and Existence — A Comparative Research Synthesis

Consciousness Project

Project direction: Michael Nemeth

AI-assisted draft synthesis. Project review does not constitute external peer review.

Version 0.2.0 · 2026-09-13

Draft: P2R20 and P2G2 pending

Abstract

This working draft examines whether fundamental, enduring consciousness offers a better account of existence, experience, meaning and morality than developed alternatives. Phase 2 improves the documentation of arguments, criticisms, replies and empirical limitations. It does not establish the proposed package. Nine component claims remain distinct; six explanatory families receive common assessment criteria. Eighteen named sensitivity scenarios clarify which arguments depend on particular cases, philosophical premises, reporting assumptions and source availability. All twelve stronger-conclusion gates remain withheld. The most useful result is a more discriminating account of what the selected evidence supports, what remains conditional, and what further observation or argument could change the assessment. [1] [2]

1. The question and the provisional answer

The motivating proposal connects several questions: why anything concrete exists, why experience accompanies physical processes, whether persons continue beyond death, and whether lives possess lasting purpose or moral direction. A conscious foundation might address several together. Its explanatory attraction depends, however, on justified connections between them. Experience being fundamental would not automatically make it universal, timeless, benevolent or authoritative.

The present assessment preserves that proposal as a serious conditional programme. It also preserves physical, emergent, experiential, dualist, idealist and theistic alternatives with different commitments. Better documentation has made their disagreements more precise. It has neither selected a global winner nor established that all rivals are equally credible. Version 1 remains a historical assessment; this draft incorporates the submitted Phase 2 synthesis and sensitivity work without representing completion of the final paper or review. [1, opening and cumulative cases]

2. Method and evidence boundaries

The project separates three judgments: reliability of a record, strength of an inference from it, and confidence in a component hypothesis. Faithfully attributing an argument does not establish its premises. Verifying that someone reported an experience does not independently verify the event described. Selected research packets pair developed positions with serious criticisms and actual replies where accessible. Versions, passage limits, missing texts and disagreements remain explicit.

Comparison uses six criteria: coherence, explanatory scope, independent warrant and cost, evidential fit, discrimination, and remaining problems or stopping points. Cases and repeated reports are grouped by their dependence. No source count becomes an evidence weight, and the synthesis is neither a meta-analysis nor an exhaustive systematic review.

At this drafting checkpoint, 41 of 46 planned tasks are accepted. That is workflow progress, not an evidential vote. P2S01 and P2S02 are submitted; a correction to P2S02 was checked for dependent drafting only. Full final review remains pending. This auxiliary draft adds no new literature search, original-source inspection or empirical data. Its crosswalk records exact supporting project statements and source locators. 3. Findings

3.1 Nine claims requiring separate support

  • H-F — Fundamentality. Developed irreducibility arguments motivate inquiry; an explanatory gap alone does not establish fundamental consciousness. A necessary ground need not be mental.
  • H-U — Universality. One grounding domain, one numerical cosmic subject and a plurality of conscious beings are different proposals. Recurring experiential motifs do not settle their relation.
  • H-B — Brain independence. Neural interventions constrain denial of neural contribution. No selected crisis case establishes the conjunction of accurate content, acquisition time and exclusion of relevant ordinary processes.
  • H-S — Personal survival. Same-person survival requires actual further subjectivity and justified identity beyond irreversible death. Survivor reports and coherent identity criteria do not establish that conjunction.
  • H-T — Timelessness. Developed eternal–temporal relations remain conditional models. Eternal representation, formal physics and altered felt time do not identify an actual timeless human subject.
  • H-P — Preservation. Preserving records, value, relationships and subjectivity are distinct achievements. A preserved history alone supplies no further experience.
  • H-I — Intention. Conscious life exists; intentional production is an additional claim. Inferring goals requires independently defended expectations and connections to their fulfilment.
  • H-G — Goodness. Specified perfect-governance proposals face substantive suffering and hiddenness objections. Possible restoration does not establish actual restoration or adequate permission for harm.
  • H-A — Moral authority. Consciousness, power, creation and common subjecthood supply no automatic normative bridge. Value, categorical applicability, overriding reasons and legitimate authority remain distinct.

These boundaries control the cumulative argument. No component inherits confirmation simply because another appears attractive. [1, H-F through H-A] [2, component assessment]

3.2 Strong alternatives and their unresolved burdens

Physical identity and physical grounding distinguish differences in concepts from differences in what exists. Frankish’s selected illusionist account retains reports, functions and introspective representations while denying phenomenal properties as conceived. Nida-Rümelin contests whether this preserves the experiential target. Identity and illusionism require separate assessment; neither an explanatory promise nor a challenge to introspection completes the explanation.

Basic psychophysical laws and causal emergence offer distinct alternatives to identity. O’Connor and Wong’s later account revises the earlier supervenience formulation toward causal maintenance. Kim’s exclusion challenge and Shoemaker’s latent-power alternative expose different causal commitments. A coherent response does not establish the actual laws or a surviving subject.

Experiential constitution and panpsychism avoid emergence from wholly nonexperiential ingredients. The inspected Strawson material and Goff’s criticism sharpen the separate problems of experiential combination, subject individuation and extending some experience to all relevant constituents. The exact missing opening article is not replaced by assumed equivalence with a later version.

Substance dualism develops distinct subjects without equating distinctness with disembodiment. Lowe’s bearer argument, Bennett’s conditional exclusion framework and Engelhardt’s later shared-property-token revision leave different causal options. A distinct bearer does not establish actual persistence after death.

Plural and universal idealism also differ. Leibniz’s many perceivers and Kastrup’s private subjects within a common mind face different individuation and common-order burdens. The selected criticisms do not amount to a complete direct exchange for every modern version.

A conscious creator can combine necessary-ground, design and moral proposals, but those contributions retain separate premises. Gale–Pruss, fine-tuning and goodness arguments do not automatically establish mentality, designer preferences, personal preservation or authority. Moral alternatives cross these six families; rejecting one naturalistic premise does not refute constructivism or robust realism. [1, six-family comparison]

3.3 What the empirical material can discriminate

Unusual reported details, identified witnesses and technical disagreements merit attention. Reynolds, the dentures case, Neal and Alexander nevertheless remain existing event groups; retellings and replies add documentation rather than independent experiences. Public narratives do not become physiological measurements, and a source’s reported chart review does not give the project original chart access.

Prospective and retrospective evidence also retain distinct uses. The AWARE II community arm cannot inherit prospective EEG observations. An installed target is not an effective opportunity for a particular participant. Accurate information, opportunity, reporting time and physiological monitoring must intersect in the same case. Early Parnia response-item coding must not be substituted for a full visual out-of-body account.

Known analytic reuse is different from known participant overlap. The selected Heim material adds historical European survivor accounts, including conscious falling, rather than new non-Western coverage or another arrest sample. Neural manipulation contributes constraints at its actual measurement level; behavior, arousal and reports do not by themselves establish metaphysical identity. Conversely, incomplete neural measurement does not establish extracerebral acquisition. Possible ordinary mechanisms are not demonstrated causes of every report. 3.4 What changed under sensitivity tests

P2S02 actually executed the seventeen inherited named scenarios across eight designs, plus the additional original-restriction scenario SENS-P2G1-08B. Five supplementary diagnostic controls were counted separately. These are qualitative dependency tests, not posterior probabilities or votes for hypotheses. Each main scenario starts from the same updated baseline.

Removing Reynolds, removing dentures, and removing both withdraws their specific favorable and critical contributions without deleting unrelated prospective cohorts. Removing retrospective contributions narrows both recurrence arguments and cultural countercomparisons while retaining prospective arms. Qualifying uncertain overlap withdraws independent-convergence use without inventing duplicate-person counts or erasing descriptive observations.

Identity tests distinguish five criteria across continuation, timeless-foundation and history-preservation possibilities. A valuable successor remains different from strict identity; records alone are not further subjectivity. Removing the premise that all significance requires endurance preserves finite fitting engagement. Removing unity-to-obligation or a specified authority bridge does not entail moral nihilism.

Removing explanatory, modal or designer-expectation premises blocks the corresponding inference. It does not prove the rival account. Missing numerical likelihoods do not eliminate every qualitative evidential relation. Human-report qualifications apply to favorable crisis narratives and neural experiments alike; animal-arousal proxies receive their own qualification.

The corrected original-restriction tests separate available source passages from metadata, access events, transcript text and project reasoning. Missing or stopped source bodies supply no original-reading credit. Removing unsupported extensions narrows permitted uses while retaining valid limitation records. The broad component labels and all twelve gates remain unchanged, although particular arguments lose force. Additional controls preserve differences between Railton, Korsgaard and Enoch, and between physical identity and other causal or representational variants. [2, executed effects and correction]

4. The strongest cumulative case and countercase

The strongest positive reconstruction begins with experience as something requiring explanation and asks whether one constrained conscious basis could account for lawful appearances and distinct persons. Independently established anomalous acquisition could challenge a specified ordinary-process account. Independently constrained agency expectations could add force from life and order. Actual worthwhile same-person continuation could connect intended development with lasting goods. These potential connections deserve assessment together, but none is earned by assuming the proposed ground, survival or goodness.

The strongest countercase combines developed alternatives with observed neural constraints, missing clinical intersections, source dependence and unresolved identity relations. Suffering and nonresistant nonbelief challenge specified perfectly good or loving governance. Finite fitting engagement challenges the necessity of endurance for every kind of meaning. If unknown reasons answer every discrepancy, positive recognition of goodness also needs justification; this condition does not adjudicate every skeptical-theist approach.

Phase 2 therefore changes the quality of the comparison more than its overall conclusion. Defenders have more precise replies; critics face those replies rather than simplified targets. Neither improvement settles the disputed premises. Explanation of concrete existence, enduring personal significance, finite significance and the integrated package remain separate synthesis questions. 5. Withheld conclusions and remaining work

All twelve conclusion gates remain withheld. Their prohibited stronger conclusions concern:

  1. A strongest-version winner across all six explanatory families.
  2. Elimination of strong moral rivals or uniquely conscious obligation.
  3. AWARE settling extracerebral acquisition or corrected pooled rates.
  4. Reynolds or dentures independently verifying perception under excluded ordinary processes.
  5. An established earliest Neal prediction or independently timed Alexander acquisition.
  6. Global NDE motif invariance, prevalence or independent pooled convergence.
  7. A winning neural theory, complete explanation or metaphysical identity.
  8. Established necessary consciousness or an intentional creator.
  9. Empirically established same-person survival or an actual timeless bearer.
  10. Endurance necessary for all meaning or an actual cosmic preserving purpose.
  11. Established good governance, victim restoration or complete defeat of evil and hiddenness objections.
  12. Exhaustive literature coverage or reconstructed historical searches.

These gates combine source, measurement and inference requirements. Obtaining a missing text may repair an attribution without closing its associated empirical or philosophical gap. Uncollated editions, unauthenticated audio, unknown effective trials and missing person-specific clinical intersections remain consequential. The next improvements should target those distinctions; publication of this draft grants no scientific acceptance. [3, retained gate records]

6. Provisional conclusion

The selected work supports a more careful investigation of conscious foundations, not an established integrated answer. It preserves experiential questions, substantive neural constraints, serious survivor reports and developed philosophical alternatives without collapsing their evidential levels. Better discrimination requires explicit predictions, warranted connecting premises and appropriately linked observations. This working release makes the present reasoning available for correction and improvement while P2R20 and P2G2 remain pending.
