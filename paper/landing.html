Consciousness and Existence Project

A research project examining whether consciousness belongs to the foundation of reality — and what that would, and would not, mean for existence, survival and significance.

Version 0.4.6 · 2026-09-15 · Research article, AI-assisted, project direction Michael Nemeth

This is a versioned research site, and nothing on it is externally peer reviewed. The research article is an authorized working draft (version 0.4.6). The thesis that expands it is released as version 1.0.1: every part was drafted, independently reviewed and accepted within the project's recorded procedure, and its coverage gate, whole-thesis review and release adjudication are closed with recorded decisions, which also close the project's final internal review steps (P2R20 and P2G2). Acceptance is methodological and establishes no metaphysical result; all twelve stronger conclusions that the project withheld remain withheld.
