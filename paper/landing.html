Where the evidence stands

The ladder of claims

Each rung up is a bigger claim, and each needs support the rung below cannot supply on its own. The verdict is asymmetric: strong grounds that embodied brains matter to experience, a genuinely open question about why experience exists, and a grand unifying picture that remains possible, attractive and unproven.

  4. 3Serious, contestedExperience belongs in reality's basic inventory.
  5. 2Open puzzleWhy physical processes are experienced at all.
  6. 1Well-supportedBrain activity causally shapes what is experienced. Controlled intervention evidence supports this, within the scope of the single case studied.

Status of this release

What has, and has not, been reviewed

This is a versioned research site, and nothing on it is externally peer reviewed. The research article is an authorized working draft (version 0.4.7). The thesis that expands it is released as version 1.0.2: every part was drafted, independently reviewed and accepted within the project's recorded procedure, and its coverage gate, whole-thesis review and release adjudication are closed with recorded decisions, which also close the project's final internal review steps (P2R20 and P2G2).

Acceptance is methodological and establishes no metaphysical result; all twelve stronger conclusions that the project withheld remain withheld. Research article, AI-assisted; project direction Michael Nemeth.
