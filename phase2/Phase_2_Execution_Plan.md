## Budgets and stopping rules

Each research packet starts with up to12search queries and8source inspections. Reserve query capacity for separate original-specific forward passes; backward-reference reading is logged separately and any additional searches count. Aim for a defensible selected defender/challenge/reply chain, not eight papers merely to fill a quota. Each paired review starts with4queries and8inspections. Root may approve one bounded extension of4queries/4inspections with a stated need. At most2substantive revision cycles per task precede explicit adjudication, narrowing or a separately recorded scope amendment.

The baseline task budgets total312search queries and560source-inspection actions, including packet reviews, audits and the final review. They are ceilings, not forecasts, unique-paper counts or targets. Reused sources still require independent review of decisive passages. No elapsed-time estimate is implied.

The final-review budget of240source/version inspections is **provisional**. P2G1 must compute the actual combined decisive inventory, including author extensions and source versions. Root can record one increase up to320before review if needed. If that remains insufficient, revise the plan or withhold the affected conclusions explicitly; never silently skip originals to fit a cap.

Do not create endless new packets because a philosophical dispute persists. Additional work is justified by a concrete missing source, material defect or decision-changing gap. Broad new topics require a numbered plan amendment with revised totals.

## Evidence integrity and change control

Freeze each task’s inputs at dispatch. Root coordinates source identities and acquisition ownership, merges only reviewed proposals, and preserves dissent. A changed accepted claim creates an impact record identifying dependent claims, arguments, routes, components and synthesis sections. Affected concurrent packets require a documented rebase before acceptance; dependent synthesis is blocked until reassessed. Version1 remains a frozen historical release.

Carry three separate assessments throughout: reliability of the record, strength of inference and confidence in each hypothesis component. Preserve event/sample/source dependence, target denominators, narrative timing, identity conditions and descriptive/normative distinctions. Do not infer probabilities from source counts or infer truth from a completed argument graph.

## Continuous execution and recovery

Phase 2 research has not launched. On research continuation, dispatch P2T01/P2T02 and queue P2T06/P2T07 and pursue eligible work through **P2G2** without asking for permission after every packet. Progress updates and DOI requests continue while other work advances.

Execution occurs during active sessions. It is not a background service and cannot promise work after the session ends. At every submission, review or adjudication, save task status, input hashes, decisions and exact next eligible work. On interruption, verify the manifest, reconcile actual agent liveness, inspect saved packets and resume the queue without repeating completed research. The ready queue and final gate replace the previous one-packet-per-continuation stopping pattern.
